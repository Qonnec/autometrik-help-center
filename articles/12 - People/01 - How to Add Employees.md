# How to Add Employees

Use the following procedure to **add an employee.**

1.  **Navigate to the People** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Employees**" under the People menu.

3.  Click the "**New**" button at the top right.

4.  Fill in the required fields under "Employee Information":

<!-- end list -->

  - Enter the **First Name**.

  - Enter the **Last Name**.

  - Set the **Birthday**.

  - Set the **Date Hired**.

  - Enter the **Employee** **No**.

  - Select the **Designation**.

  - Assign a **User Account**.

5\. Fill in the required fields under "Contact Information":

  - Enter the **Mobile** **No**.

  - Enter the **Email Address**.

  - Enter the **Address** (optional).

6\. Click "**Add Employee**" to save the new employee record.
