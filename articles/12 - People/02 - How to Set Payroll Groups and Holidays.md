# How to Set Payroll Groups and Holidays

Use the following procedure to **set a holiday.**

1.  **Navigate to the People** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Holidays**" under the People menu.

3.  Click the "**New**" button at the top right to create a new holiday.

4.  Fill in the required fields under "**Holiday Information**":

<!-- end list -->

  - Enter the **Name of the holiday**.

  - Enter a **Description** (optional).

  - Set the **Date**.

  - Select whether it is a **Regular Holiday** or **Special Holiday**.

5\. Click "**Add** **Holiday**" to save the new holiday.
