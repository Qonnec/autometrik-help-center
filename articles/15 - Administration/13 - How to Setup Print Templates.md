# How to Setup Print Templates

**ESTIMATES**

1.  **Access the Print Templates Section**

<!-- end list -->

  - Navigate to administration in the left sidebar.

  - Click print templates to open the template designer.

  - Select or create a new template (e.g., for "Estimate Layout"). The main canvas will load with the title "Estimate Layout" at the top.

2\. **Add Header Elements** (Customer and Vehicle Info)

  - From the right "fields & label" panel, drag the following fields to the top of the canvas:

<!-- end list -->

1.  label (type "label" as a placeholder).

2.  customer

3.  address

4.  make

5.  model

6.  estimated days

7.  reference no

8.  date created

9.  plate no.

<!-- end list -->

  - Arrange them in rows, aligning horizontally (e.g., customer and address in the first row, make, model, and estimated days in the next).

3\. **Add Service Section**

  - Below the header, drag service name and service total from the right panel.

  - Position service name to span most of the row, with service total at the end.

4\. **Add Parts Replaced Section**

  - Below the services, drag label and type "parts replaced".

  - Drag product name and product total.

  - Align product name to span the row, with product total at the end.

5\. **Add Subtotal and Total Sections**

  - At the bottom of the canvas, drag:

<!-- end list -->

1.  services subtotal

2.  products subtotal

3.  total amount

<!-- end list -->

  - Align them to the right for a clean look.

  - Optionally, add sub total, tax, and discount above the totals for breakdowns.

6\. **Customize and Finalize**

  - Use label for additional custom text (e.g., headings or notes).

  - Drag other fields as needed: expiry date, product price, product quantity, or km reading.

  - Rearrange elements by dragging them on the canvas, using dotted borders for alignment.

7\. **Save and Activate**

  - Click save (typically in the top bar).

  - Toggle activate to "yes" in the top-right to set this as the default estimate template.

  - Test the layout by printing a sample estimate to verify the design.
