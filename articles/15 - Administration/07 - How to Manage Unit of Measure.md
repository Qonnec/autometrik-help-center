# How to Manage Unit of Measure

Use the following procedure to **manage unit of measure.**

1.  On the left sidebar, locate and click on **Administration**.

2.  From the expanded menu, click on **Units of Measure**.

3.  On the Units of Measure page, you will see a **list of existing units** with details like Unit Code, Name, and Description.

4.  **To edit an existing unit**, **click on the unit name**, then update the Unit Code, Name, and Description fields as needed.

5.  **To add a new unit**, click the **New** button in the top-right corner, enter the Unit Code, Name, and Description, and click Add Unit to save.

6.  Click **Save Unit** to save any changes to an existing unit.
