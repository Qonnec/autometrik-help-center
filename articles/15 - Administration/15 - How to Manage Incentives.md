# How to Manage Incentives

The **Incentives Feature** in Autometrik is designed to motivate your team and streamline your commission structures. By automating the calculation of payouts for service advisors, sales personnel, and administrative staff, you ensure transparency and accuracy in rewarding your team for every billed service or product sale.

**Purpose of Incentives:**

Incentives serve as a powerful tool to drive productivity within your shop or dealership. By setting up pre-defined "mechanics" for rewards, Autometrik automatically tracks commissions as you bill customers. This eliminates manual bookkeeping and provides staff with a clear view of their earned rewards, fostering a high-performance environment.

**Step 1: Set Up Your Incentive Records**

Before rewards can be tracked, you must define the incentive logic.

1.  Navigate to **Administration** \> **Incentives**.

2.  Enter the name for the record (e.g., "Standard Incentive").

3.  **Define Payouts**: Enter the specific amounts or percentages for each role:
    
      - **Mechanic**: The reward for the technician performing the labor.
    
      - **Sales**: This applies to the **Service** **Advisor** within the Job Order module and the **Sales Person** within the Sales Order module.
    
      - **Admin**: The reward for administrative or support staff.

4.  **Value Type**: Use the toggle to switch between a flat fee (\#) or a percentage (%).

5.  **Max** **Value**: Set a cap in the Max Value field to ensure the payout does not exceed a specific limit per item.

6.  **Save** the record.

**Step 2: Assign Incentives to Products & Services**

Once the records are created, you must link them to the items you want to track.

1.  Navigate to **Administration** \> **Incentives**. Navigate to your Product or Service list.

2.  Open the details for the specific item.

3.  Locate the **Incentive** dropdown field.

4.  Select the relevant incentive record from the list.

5.  **Save** the changes.

\[***NOTE***: Incentives are triggered automatically every time you bill a customer for these assigned products or services.\]

**Step 3: Tracking via Incentive Reports**

To view performance and calculate payouts, use the dedicated reporting tools.

1.  Navigate to **Reports** \> **Incentive Reports**.

2.  **Filter by Date Range**: Select the period you wish to review.

3.  **Select View Type**: Choose the specific category of incentives you wish to see:
    
      - Mechanic Incentives
    
      - Sales Incentives
    
      - Admin Incentives

4.  The report will display the **accumulated incentives** per staff member based on the billed transactions.
