# How to Manage Product Pricing

Use the following procedure to **manage product pricing.**

1.  On the left sidebar, locate and click on **Administration**.

2.  From the expanded menu, click on **Product Pricing**.

3.  On the Product Pricing page, **you will see a list of existing products** with details like Product Code, Name, Category, Unit, Supplier, Cost, and Price.

4.  **To edit an existing product's pricing**, **click on the product name**, then update the cost and price fields as needed.

5.  **To add a new product pricing**, click the **New** button in the top-right corner, enter the Product Code, Name, Category, Unit, Supplier, Cost, and Price, and click Add Product Pricing to save.

6.  Click **Save Product Pricing** to save any changes to an existing product's pricing.
