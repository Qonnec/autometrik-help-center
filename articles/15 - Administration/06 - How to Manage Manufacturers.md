# How to Manage Manufacturers

Use the following procedure to **manage manufacturers.**

1.  On the left sidebar, locate and click on **Administration**.

2.  From the expanded menu, click on **Manufacturers**.

3.  On the Manufacturers page, you will see a list of existing manufacturers with details like Manufacturer Name, Contact Person, Phone, Email, and Address.

4.  To edit an existing manufacturer, click on the manufacturer name, then update the Name, Contact Person, Phone, Email, and Address fields as needed.

5.  To add a new manufacturer, click the **New** button in the top-right corner, enter the required details, and click **Add Manufacturer** to save.

6.  Click **Save Manufacturer** to save any changes to an existing manufacturer.
