# How to Manage Product Groups

Use the following procedure to **manage product groups.**

1.  On the left sidebar, locate and click on **Administration**.

2.  From the expanded menu, click on **Product Groups**.

3.  On the Product Groups page, **you will see a list of existing product** groups with details like Group Name and Description.

4.  **To edit an existing product group**, click on the group name, **then update** the Name and Description fields as needed.

5.  **To add a new product group**, click the **New** button in the top-right corner, enter the Name and Description, and click Add Product Group to save.

6.  Click **Save Product Group** to save any changes to an existing group.
