# How to Manage Warranty Templates

Use the following procedure to **manage templates.**

1.  **Navigation**: From the main dashboard, click on Administration in the left-hand menu, then select Warranty Templates.

2.  **View Templates**: On the Warranty Templates page, you will see a list of existing templates, including the name, date, and action options.

3.  **Create New Template**: To add a new warranty template, click the New button in the top right corner.

4.  **Enter Template Details**: Fill in the required fields such as the template name, description, covered services, covered products, and terms & conditions.

5.  **Add Covered Services**: In the Covered Services section, click + Add Service to include specific services (e.g., Shock Absorber Replacement, Shock Absorber Initial Inspection and Alignment).

6.  **Add Covered Products**: In the Covered Products section, click + Add Product to include specific products (e.g., NITROTECH Shock Absorber Front Left, NITROTECH Shock Absorber Front Right).

7.  **Set Terms & Conditions**: Input the warranty terms and conditions in the designated text area, including duration, exclusions, and repair conditions.

8.  **Save Template**: Once all details are entered, click the Save button to store the new warranty template.

9.  **Edit or Delete**: To modify an existing template, click the action icon (three dots) next to the template name and select Edit or Delete as needed.

10. **Print Template**: To generate a printable version, select a template and click the action icon, then choose Print to view and print the warranty document.
