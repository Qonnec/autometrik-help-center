# How to Manage Inspection Templates

Use the following procedure to **manage templates.**

1.  On the left sidebar, locate and click on **Administration**.

2.  From the expanded menu, click on **Inspection Templates**.

3.  On the Inspection Templates page, **you will see a list of existing templates** (e.g., "Inspection Report," "Test Templates").

4.  **To edit** an existing template, click on its name to view details, then modify the selected groups, options (e.g., Paper Size, Text Size, Columns, Notes Placement), and settings (e.g., Hide Status Label, Enable Diagnostic Results).

5.  **To add** a new template, click the **New** button in the top-right corner, enter a name, select desired groups from "Available Groups" using the Add Group button, and configure the options and settings.

6.  Click **Save Inspection Template** or to save your changes or create the new template.
