# How to Manage Vehicle Makes and Model

Use the following procedure to **manage vehicle makes.**

1.  **Navigation**: From the main dashboard, click on Administration in the left-hand menu, then select Vehicle Makes from the submenu.

2.  **Access Details**: Once on the Vehicle Makes page, locate the list section where you can view and edit the vehicle make entries.

3.  **Review Existing Makes**: Check the current list of vehicle makes displayed in the table, including details like Name and Date.

4.  **Edit a Vehicle Make (if needed):** Click on an existing vehicle make to open its details, then modify the Name or Description fields as required.

5.  **Add a New Vehicle Make (if needed):** Click the "New" button and enter a new Name and optional Description for the vehicle make.

6.  **Enable/Disable Makes (if applicable):** Ensure the vehicle make is active; if a toggle or status option exists, adjust it to enable or disable the entry as needed.

7.  **Save Changes**: After making any adjustments, click the "Save" or "Save Vehicle Make" button to apply the changes.

8.  **Verify**: Confirm that the updated or new vehicle makes are reflected in the list by refreshing the page or checking against existing records.

Use the following procedure to **manage vehicle models.**

1.  **Navigation**: From the main dashboard, click on Administration in the left-hand menu, then select Vehicle Models from the submenu.

2.  **Access Details**: Once on the Vehicle Models page, locate the list section where you can view and edit the vehicle model entries.

3.  **Review Existing Models**: Check the current list of vehicle models displayed in the table, including details like Model Name, Make, and Date.

4.  **Edit a Vehicle Model (if needed):** Click on an existing vehicle model to open its details, then modify the Model Name or associated Make fields as required.

5.  **Add a New Vehicle Model (if needed)**: Click the "New" button and enter a new Model Name, then select or link it to an existing Vehicle Make from the dropdown or list.

6.  **Enable/Disable Models (if applicable):** Ensure the vehicle model is active; if a toggle or status option exists, adjust it to enable or disable the entry as needed.

7.  **Save Changes**: After making any adjustments, click the "Save" or "Save Vehicle Model" button to apply the changes.

8.  **Verify**: Confirm that the updated or new vehicle models are reflected in the list by refreshing the page or checking against existing records.
