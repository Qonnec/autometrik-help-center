# How to Access Service Category

Use the following procedure to **manage service category.**

1.  On the left sidebar, locate and click on **Administration**.

2.  From the expanded menu, click on **Service Categories**.

3.  On the Service Categories page, click the **New** button in the top-right corner.

4.  Fill in the **Name** and **Description** fields with the desired information for the new service category.

5.  If applicable, select a Parent Category from the dropdown menu or leave it as "No".

6.  Click the **Add Service Category** button to save the new category.
