# How to Manage Pricing Tiers

Pricing tiers refer to a **structured system of setting different price levels for products, services, or subscriptions based on factors like volume, features, user type, or commitment level**. This approach allows administrators to cater to diverse customer needs, optimize revenue, and encourage specific purchasing behaviors, such as offering discounts for higher quantities or premium features for a higher tier.

Use the following procedure to **manage pricing tiers.**

1.  **Access Pricing Settings**: Log in to the administration dashboard and navigate to the "Pricing Tiers" section, found under a main menu like "Administration”.

2.  **View Existing Tiers**: On the Pricing page, review the current pricing tiers listed in a table or grid. This may include columns for Tier Name, Price, Features, Volume Limits, or Status (e.g., Active or Inactive).

3.  **Create or Edit a Tier**: Click "Add New Tier" or select an existing tier to edit. Enter details such as Tier Name (e.g., Basic, Pro, Enterprise), Price, Applicable Features, Minimum/Maximum Volume, and any Discount Rates or Conditions.

4.  **Set Rules and Conditions**: Define eligibility criteria (e.g., customer type, purchase quantity) and adjust pricing dynamically if supported (e.g., tiered discounts for bulk orders). Save changes to update the tier.

5.  **Assign Tiers**: Link pricing tiers to specific products, services, or customer groups via a dropdown or assignment tool. Ensure proper categorization to avoid overlap.

6.  **Test and Review**: Use a preview or simulation feature (if available) to test how tiers apply to sample transactions. Check for consistency and adjust as needed.

7.  **Publish Changes**: Once satisfied, activate or save the updated tiers. Monitor performance through reports or analytics to refine tiers over time.
