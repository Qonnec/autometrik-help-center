# How to Access Membership and Loyalty Programs

Use the following procedure to **manage membership.**

1.  On the left sidebar, locate and click on **Administration**.

2.  From the expanded menu, click on **Memberships**.

3.  On the Memberships page, you will see a **list of existing memberships** with details like Membership No, Name, Membership Date, Expiry Date, Mobile No, Email, Location, Status, and Date.

4.  **To edit an existing membership**, **click on the membership details to view** and **update** the Membership Information (e.g., Membership No, Points Earned, Membership Date, Expiry Date, Location, Active status), Personal Information (e.g., First Name, Last Name, Birth Date), and Contact Information (e.g., Mobile No, Email Address, Address).

5.  **To add a new membership**, click the **New** button in the top-right corner, fill in the required fields, and click Add Membership to save.

6.  Click **Save Membership** to save any changes to an existing membership.
