# How to Manage Income Statements

An **Income Statement** is a **financial report that shows a company's revenues, expenses, and profits or losses over a specific period.** It helps assess the business's financial performance by listing accounts like revenue and cost of goods sold with their balances.

Use the following procedure to **access income statements.**

1.  From the left sidebar menu, click on **Accounting** to expand the section.

2.  Select **Income Statement** from the Accounting submenu.

3.  In the Report Filter section, **enter the desired date range** (e.g., "From" 1 Jul 2025 to "To" 31 Jul 2025).

4.  Click the **Generate Report** button to view the Income Statement for the specified period.
