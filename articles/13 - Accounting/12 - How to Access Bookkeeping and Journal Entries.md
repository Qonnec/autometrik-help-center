# How to Access Bookkeeping and Journal Entries

Use the following procedure to **managed journal entries.**

1.  **Navigate to the "Accounting"** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Journal Entry**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  On the "JOURNAL ENTRY" page, click the "**New**" button.

4.  In the "NEW JOURNAL ENTRY" form:

<!-- end list -->

  - Enter the "**Date**."

  - Input the "**Reference**."

  - Add a "**Description**."

5\. In the "Entry" section, add lines with:

  - "**Account**."

  - "**Debit**" or "**Credit**" amount.

6**. Ensure total debits** equal total credits.

7**. Review** the entries for accuracy.

8\. Click the "**Save**" button to record the journal entry.
