# How to Monitor All Cash Inflows and Outflows

| **Name**     | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Cash Inflow  | Refers to money coming into the business, such as revenue from sales, customer payments, loans received, or investments. It increases the available cash balance, e.g., a 1,000 payment received at 09:09 PM PST on August 09, 2025. |
| Cash Outflow | Refers to money going out of the business, such as payments to suppliers, employee salaries, or operating expenses. It decreases the available cash balance, e.g., a 500 supplier payment made at the same time.                     |

Use the following procedure to **generate a cash flow statement report.**

1.  **Navigate to the "Accounting"** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Cash Flow Statement**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  In the "**Report Filter**" section:

<!-- end list -->

  - Set the "**From**" date.

  - Set the "**To**" date.

4\. **Review the selected date range** to ensure accuracy.

5\. Click the "**Generate Report**" button to create the report.
