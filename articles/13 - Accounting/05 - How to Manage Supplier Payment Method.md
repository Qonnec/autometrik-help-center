# How to Manage Supplier Payment Method

Use the following procedure to **add a new payment method for supplier.**

1.  Navigate to the "**Accounting**" section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Supplier Payment Methods**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  On the "Supplier Payment Methods" page, click the "**New**" button.

4.  In the "**New Supplier Payment Method**" form:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Enter the name of the new payment method** in the "Name" field.

  - **Select an account** from the "Account" dropdown menu (e.g., "Type-in the account name").

  - **Provide a description** in the "Description" field (optional).

  - Set "**Track Reference**" to "Yes" or "No" as needed.

  - Set "**Check Payment**" to "Yes" or "No" as needed.

5\. **Review the entered information** to ensure accuracy.

6\. Click the "**Add Payment Method**" button to save the new payment method.
