# What are General Ledger and Trial Balance?

A **General Ledger** is a **complete record of all financial transactions of a business, organized by accounts.** It serves as the central repository for accounting data, allowing for tracking and summarizing financial activities over a specific period.

Use the following procedure to **access general ledger.**

1.  From the left sidebar menu, click on **Accounting** to expand the section.

2.  On the Accounting page, locate and **click on General Ledger**.

3.  In the Report Filter section, **enter the desired date range** (e.g., "From" 1 Jul 2025 to "To" 8 Jul 2025).

4.  Click the **Generate Report** button to view the General Ledger for the specified period.

A **Trial Balance** is a **report that lists the balances of all general ledger accounts at a specific point in time**. It ensures that total debits equal total credits, helping to detect accounting errors.

Use the following procedure to **access trial balance.**

1.  From the left sidebar menu, click on **Accounting** to expand the section.

2.  Select **Trial Balance** from the Accounting submenu.

3.  In the Report Filter section, **enter the desired date** (e.g., "To" 8 Jul 2025).

4.  Click the **Generate Report** button to view the Trial Balance as of the specified date.
