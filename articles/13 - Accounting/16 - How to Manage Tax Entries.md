# How to Manage Tax Entries

Use the following procedure to **access taxes.**

1.  **Access Accounting** on the side panel

2.  **Select Taxes** from the dropdown

3.  Initiate **new tax creation**

4.  Locate and click the **New** **button** in the "Taxes" section

5.  **Fill in the "New Tax" form** that appears:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Enter the Name** of the new tax (e.g., "Withholding Tax" or another relevant name)

  - **Input the Rate** as a decimal (e.g., 0.00 for 0%, 1.00 for 100%, etc.)

  - **Set the No field** if applicable

  - **Add a Description** to provide additional details about the tax

6\. Click the **Add Sales** **Tax** button to save the new tax entry

7\. **Return to the "Taxes**" list **to verify** the new tax appears with the correct name, rate, and date
