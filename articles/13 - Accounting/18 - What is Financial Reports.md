# What is Financial Reports?

Financial reports are **documents that show how a business is doing financially.** They help you understand how much money the business is making, spending, and what it owns or owes. These reports are usually prepared every month, quarter, or year.

  - **Income Statement** – Shows how much money the business earned and spent. It tells you if the business made a profit or a loss.

  - **Balance** **Sheet** – Lists everything the business owns (assets), owes (liabilities), and the owner’s share (equity) at a specific point in time.

  - **Cash Flow Statement** – Tracks the cash going in and out of the business. It helps you see if the business has enough cash to pay its bills.

  - **Statement of Changes in Equity** – Shows how the owner’s investment in the business has changed over time.
