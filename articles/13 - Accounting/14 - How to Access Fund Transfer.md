# How to Access Fund Transfer

Use the following procedure to **managed fund transfers.**

1.  **Navigate to the "Accounting"** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Fund Transfer**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  On the "Fund Transfer" page, click the "**New**" button.

4.  In the "New Fund Transfer" form:

<!-- end list -->

  - Select the "**From** **Account**."

  - Select the "**To Account**."

  - Enter the "**Amount**" to transfer.

  - Set the "**Date**."

  - Provide a "**Reference**" (optional).

  - Add a "**Description**" (optional).

5\. **Review the entered information** to ensure accuracy.

6\. Click the "**Transfer**" button to complete the fund transfer.
