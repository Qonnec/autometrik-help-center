# How to Access Tax Groups

Use the following procedure to **add a new tax group.**

1.  **Navigate to the "Accounting"** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Taxes**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  On the "Taxes" page, click the "**New**" button.

4.  In the "New Tax" form:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Enter the name of the new tax** in the "Name" field.

  - **Set the tax rate** in the "Rate" field (e.g., 0.00).

  - Select "**Yes**" or "**No**" for "Withholding Tax" as needed.

  - **Provide a description** in the "Description" field (optional).

5**. Review the entered information** to ensure accuracy.

6\. Click the "**Add Sales Tax**" button to save the new tax.
