# How to Manage Account Transactions

Use the following procedure to **access account transactions.**

1.  **Navigate to the "Accounting"** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Account Transactions**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  On the "Account Transactions" page, **select the desired account** from the list.

4.  **Use the filters** (e.g., Date Range, Transaction Type) to narrow down transactions.

5.  **Review the transaction** **details**, including Date, Type, Description, Debit, Credit, and Balance.

6.  **Click on a specific transaction** to view or edit its details if needed.

7.  **To add a new transaction**, click the **"New" button** and fill in the required fields (e.g., Date, Account, Amount, Description).

8.  **Save or update the transaction** as necessary.
