# How to Manage Check Received

Use the following procedure to **managed received checks.**

1.  **Navigate to the "Accounting"** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Check Received**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  On the "Check Received" page, **locate the desired check** from the list (e.g., by Check No, Customer, or Amount).

4.  Click on the check entry to **view its details**.

5.  In the "**Check Details**" section:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Verify** the "Check No," "Check Date," "Date Cleared," "Customer," "Amount," and "Status."

  - **Update** "Particulars" if needed (e.g., invoice number).

  - Click "**Clear Check**" to mark the check as cleared if applicable.

  - Click "**View Payment Details**" or "**View Expense Details**" for additional information.

6**. Review the details** and make any necessary adjustments.
