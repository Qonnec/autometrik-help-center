# How to Manage Types of Accounts

Use the following procedure to **manage type of accounts.**

1.  Navigate to the "**Accounting**" section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Type of Accounts**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  On the "Type of Accounts" page, locate and click the "**Create**" option.

4.  In the "**New Account Type**" form:

<!-- end list -->

  - Enter the name of the new account type in the "Name" field.

  - Enter a prefix for the account type in the "Prefix" field.

  - Provide a description in the "Description" field (optional).

5\. **Review the entered information** to ensure accuracy.

6\. Click the "**Add Account Type**" button to save the new account type.
