# How to Manage Balance Sheets

A **Balance Sheet** is a **financial statement that provides a snapshot of a company's assets, liabilities, and equity at a specific point in time**, showing what the business owns and owes. It helps assess financial health.

Use the following procedure to **access balance sheets.**

1.  From the left sidebar menu, click on **Accounting** to expand the section.

2.  Select **Balance Sheet** from the Accounting submenu.

3.  In the Report Filter section, **enter the desired date** (e.g., "To" 8 Jul 2025).

4.  Click the **Generate Report** button to view the Balance Sheet as of the specified date.
