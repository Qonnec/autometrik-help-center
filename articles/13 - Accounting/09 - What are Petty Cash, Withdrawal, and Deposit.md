# What are Petty Cash, Withdrawal, and Deposit?

| **Name**   | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Petty Cash | A small amount of cash kept on hand by a business to cover minor, day-to-day expenses such as office supplies or small reimbursements, typically managed through a petty cash fund. |
| Withdrawal | The act of taking money out of an account or fund, such as removing cash from a bank account or petty cash fund for business use or personal needs.                                 |
| Deposit    | The act of adding money into an account or fund, such as placing cash or checks into a bank account or replenishing a petty cash fund.                                              |
