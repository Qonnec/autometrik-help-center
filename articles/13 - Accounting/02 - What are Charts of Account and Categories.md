# What are Charts of Account and Categories

The Chart of Accounts lists all financial accounts, including **assets** (e.g., Cash in Bank, Consigned Goods), **liabilities** (e.g., Outstanding Checks Issued), **equity** (e.g., Opening Balance Equity), **income** (e.g., Marketing Services), and **expenses** (e.g., Utilities Expense, Travel Expense), helping to organize and track financial transactions effectively.

  - **Assets**: These are resources owned by the business that have economic value, such as cash in bank accounts or consigned goods (items held for sale).

  - **Liabilities**: These are debts or obligations the business owes to others, like outstanding checks issued that need to be cleared.

  - **Equity**: This represents the owner's share in the business, including opening balance equity, which reflects the initial investment or retained earnings.

  - **Income**: This is the money earned from business activities, such as revenue from marketing services.

  - **Expenses**: These are costs incurred during business operations, like utilities expense or travel expense, which reduce profit.

Use the following procedure to **manage accounts and categories.**

1.  **Navigate to the Accounting** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Chart of Accounts**" under the Accounting menu to view the list of accounts.

3.  **Review the details** such as Code, Name, Type, Category, and Date.

4.  To add a new account, click the "**New**" button at the top right.

5.  **Fill in the required fields** for the new account (specific fields may vary, but typically include Code, Name, Type, Category, and Date).

6.  Click "**Add**" or "**Save**" to create the new account entry
