# How to Manage Checks Issued

Use the following procedure to **managed issued checks.**

1.  **Navigate to the "Accounting"** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Check Issued**" under the Accounting menu.

3.  On the "Check Issued" page, **locate the desired check** from the list (e.g., by CV No, Check No, Payee, or Amount).

4.  Click on the check entry to **view its details**.

5.  In the "Check Details" section:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Verify** the "Voucher No," "Voucher Date," "Date Cleared," "Payee," "Amount," "Check No," "Check Date," and "Status."

  - **Update** "Particulars" if needed (e.g., Bank Charges).

  - Click "**Clear Check**" to mark the check as cleared if applicable.

  - Click "**View Payment Details**" or "**View Expense Details**" for additional information.

6\. For **printing**, select a check and click "**Print**" to generate a "**Print Check Voucher**" document.

7**. Review the details** and make any necessary adjustments.
