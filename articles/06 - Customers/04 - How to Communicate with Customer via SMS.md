# How to Communicate with Customer via SMS

Use the following procedure to **send a customer an SMS**.

1.  Navigate to the **CRM** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Select **SMS Messages**.

3.  Click **Send SMS** to open the SMS creation form.

4.  **Enter** the **recipient's Mobile No**.

5.  **Type the SMS message** in the SMS Message field.

6.  Click **Send SMS** to deliver the message.

The Service Reminders feature in the Auto Shop Office system **allows users** **to** efficiently **notify customers** about upcoming vehicle maintenance or service needs. This feature helps streamline operations, enhance customer satisfaction, and keep track of service schedules with statuses like "Sent" or "Active" updated in real-time.

Use the following procedure to **send a reminder to a customer via SMS**.

1.  Navigate to the **CRM** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Select **Service Reminders**.

3.  **Locate** the service reminder you want to send.

4.  **Check the box** next to the desired reminder date and service.

5.  Click **Send Reminders** to send the reminder as an SMS to the customer's mobile number.

The system **will update the status** to Sent once the SMS is delivered
