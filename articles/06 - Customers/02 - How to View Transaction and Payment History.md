# How to View Transaction and Payment History

Use the following procedure to **view customer transaction history**.

1.  **Navigate to Customers**: Click on the "Customers" option in the left menu to open the customer management page.

2.  **Select a Vehicle**: Find the customer in the list and click on it to view their details.

3.  **Access Service and Sales History**:
    
      - Look for the "Service History" or “Sales History” tab or link within the vehicle’s page.
    
      - Click "Service History" or “Sales History” to display the full record.

4.  **Review the Details**: The service history will show:
    
      - **Reference No.**: A unique identifier for each service (e.g., R0000139).
    
      - **Services**: A list of tasks performed (e.g., Oil Top-Up, Brake Fluid Top Up).
    
      - **Amount**: The cost of each service.
    
      - **Date**: When the service was done.
    
      - **Status**: The current status (e.g., Released).
    
      - **Total**: The overall cost for all services listed.

5.  **Explore Further**: Scroll through the list to see all past services, or use filters if available to narrow down by date or status.
