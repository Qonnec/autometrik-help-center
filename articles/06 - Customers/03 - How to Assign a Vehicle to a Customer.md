# How to Assign a Vehicle to a Customer

Use the following procedure to **link a vehicle to a customer**.

1.  Go to the **Customers** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Select **Details** and choose the customer.

3.  Click on the **Vehicles** tab.

4.  Click **Create** to open the new vehicle form.

5.  **Enter the vehicle** details such as Plate No, Model, Year Model, VIN, Engine No, Transmission, Mileage Type, Registration Type, Cylinders, Color, and Insurance Expiry.

6.  Click **Add Vehicle** to save and assign it to the customer.
