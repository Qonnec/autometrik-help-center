# How to Create and Manage Customer Information

Creating customer records means **adding new customers** to the system with their details, such as name, contact info, and linked vehicles. It helps you keep track of customers, their vehicles, and service history in one place, improving efficiency and customer service.

Use the following procedure to **create a customer record**.

1.  **Navigate to Customers**: Click on the "Customers" option in the left menu to open the customer management page.

2.  **Add a New Customer**: Look for the "New" or "Add Customer" button (usually at the top right) and click it.

3.  **Enter Customer Details**: Fill in the required fields, such as:
    
      - **Name**: The customer’s full name (e.g., "John Smith").
    
      - **Code**: A unique customer code (e.g., "CUST-001").
    
      - **Email**: Their email address for contact.
    
      - **Phone**: Their phone number.
    
      - **Address** (Optional): Their physical address.
    
      - **Linked Vehicles** (Optional): Add vehicles they own (you can link later if needed).

4.  **Save the Record**: Click "Save" or "Submit" to add the customer to the system.

5.  **Verify**: Go back to the customer list to ensure the new customer appears.

Use the following procedure to **manage a customer record**.

1.  **Navigate to Customers**: Click on the "Customers" option in the left menu.

2.  **View Customers**: Browse the list to see all customers, including their names, codes, emails, and contact dates.

3.  **Edit a Record**:

  - Find the customer in the list and click on their name or the "Edit" button next to it.

  - Update details like email, phone, or linked vehicles as needed.

  - Click "Save" or "Update" to save changes.

4\. **Delete a Record (if needed)**:

  - Select the customer and look for a "Delete" option (usually a trash bin icon).

  - Confirm the deletion when prompted.

5\. **Link Vehicles**: If not done earlier, link vehicles to the customer by editing their record and adding vehicle details.

6\. **Check History**: Click on a customer to view their service history or linked vehicles for more details.
