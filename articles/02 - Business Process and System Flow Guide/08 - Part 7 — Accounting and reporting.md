# Part 7 — Accounting and reporting

Operational documents post into the books; you do not journalise sales by hand.

| **Journal**               | **Fed by**                           |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Sales Journal             | Invoices and quick sales             |
| Purchase Journal          | Supplier invoices                    |
| Cash Receipt Journal      | Customer payments and deposits       |
| Cash Disbursement Journal | Supplier payments, expenses, payroll |
| General Journal           | Manual entries and adjustments       |

From these come the Trial Balance, the General Ledger and the three statements — Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement.

The Reports menu is organised by question rather than by module: sales by product or service, daily collections, mechanic jobs and clock in/out, vehicles due for service, top customers, top suppliers, and the loyalty and incentive reports.
