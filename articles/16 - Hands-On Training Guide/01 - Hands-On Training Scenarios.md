# Hands-On Training Scenarios

1\. **The Standard Repair Cycle**

**Scenario**: A customer walks in complaining about a rough engine. You diagnose it as a **Spark Plug** issue. They agree to the price, you finish the work, and they pay the full amount in cash on the spot.

• **Step 1**: Create an **Estimate** for "Spark Plug Replacement" (Labor + Parts).

- **Step 2**: Once approved, convert the Estimate into a **Job Order**.

- **Step 3**: Finalize the work and click **Generate Invoice**.

- **Step 4**: Go to **Receive Payment**, select **Cash**, and record the full amount.

2\. **Express Services**

**Scenario**: A customer is in a hurry and only wants the **Xpress Package Regular Oil** change. They want to pay via **GCash** before leaving.

• **Step 1**: Create a new transaction. Select the "Xpress Package - Regular Oil" **from the package list**.

- **Step 2**: Move straight to **Billing**.

- **Step 3**: Record the payment and select **GCash** as the Payment Mode.

3\. **Bulk Sales & Split Payments**

**Scenario**: A customer buys **4 new tires with installation**. To close the deal, you offer a **5% discount**. The customer pays half in **Cash** and the other half via **GCash**.

• **Step 1**: Add 4 Tires and the **Installation Service** to the sales module.

- **Step 2**: Locate the **Discount** field and input **5%**.

- **Step 3**: Use the **Split Payment** feature: Input 50% under **Cash** and 50% under **GCash**.

4\. **Fleet Accounts & Withholding Tax**

**Scenario**: A **Fleet Account** brings in a vehicle. They are a withholding agent. Because they are a corporate account, they don't pay immediately—they have a **30-day window** to settle the bill.

• **Step 1**: Select a customer designated as a **Fleet Account**.

- **Step 2**: **Select** 1% EWT for products and 2% EWT for services.

- **Step 3**: The **30-day term** will be **applied automatically** based on the settings in the Customer Details.

5\. **Mid-Job Revision**

**Scenario**: You billed a customer for **2 Brake Pads**. After installation, they decide they also want a **Wheel Alignment**. You need to go back and add this new service to the bill.

• **Step 1**: Bill the customer for the 2 Brake Pads.

- **Step 2**: When the customer adds Wheel Alignment, **Cancel/Void** the current Invoice.

- **Step 3**: Change the Job Order status from C**ompleted back to In-Process** to add the new service, then re-bill.

6\. **Special Orders & Deposits**

**Scenario**: A car needs a new **AC Compressor**, but you don't have it in the shop. It will take two days to arrive. The customer leaves the car but pays a **40% deposit** to secure the part.

• **Step 1**: Create a Job Order for the AC Compressor.

- **Step 2**: Use the **Record Deposit** button to input the 40% downpayment. This credit stays on the account until the part arrives.

7\. **Inventory: Stock Adjustment**

**Scenario**: While doing a shelf count, you realize a **Wiper Blade** in your box is defective. You need to remove it from your digital inventory so your counts stay accurate.

• **Step 1**: Navigate to **Inventory Adjustments**.

- **Step 2**: Select "Wiper Blade" and perform a **Decrease Adjustment** with the reason "Defective."

8\. **Outsourcing Parts**

**Scenario**: A customer needs an **AC Condenser** repair. You have zero stock. Your mechanic knows a nearby supplier who has it. You need to order it specifically for this job.

• Step 1: Create the **Job Order**.

• Step 2: Click **Create Purchase Order (PO)** directly from the Job Order to link the external purchase to this specific repair.

9\. **Over-the-Counter Retail**

**Scenario**: A walk-in customer just wants to buy a **Wiper Blade**. He doesn't want installation; he just wants the part and the receipt.

• **Step 1**: Create a **Sales Order** (Retail/Counter Sale mode).

- **Step 2**: Convert the Sales Order to an **Invoice**.

- **Step 3**: Process the payment immediately without assigning a mechanic.

10\. **Partial Receiving & Price Changes**

**Scenario**: You ordered **50 Oil Filters**, but the supplier only delivered **45** and the price changed. You need to record exactly what arrived and the new cost.

• **Step 1**: Open the **PO** for 50 Oil Filters.

- **Step 2**: Upon delivery, **manually receive** only 45 units.

- **Step 3**: Update the **Unit Cost** in the receiving screen to reflect the new supplier price before saving.

11\. **Supplier Returns**

**Scenario**: You bought **5 Air Filters**, but later found **1 was defective**. You are sending it back to the supplier for a replacement.

• **Step 1**: Go to **Purchase Returns**.

- **Step 2**: Select the specific vendor and the 1 defective Air Filter to remove it from stock and record the return.

12\. **Warranty & Replacement**

**Scenario**: The customer from **Scenario 9** returns. The Wiper Blade he bought is defective. You agree to replace it for free. You must void the old sale and record a new one at **no cost**.

• **Step 1**: **Cancel/Void** the original Sales Order and Payment for Customer \#9.

- **Step 2**: Create a **New Sales Order** for the replacement Wiper Blade.

- **Step 3**: Manually set the **Price to 0** to reflect a free warranty replacement while keeping inventory accurate.

13\. **Recording Utilities (Electricity Bill)**

**Scenario**: You received your monthly electricity bill from **Meralco** for **9,000**. You need to record this as an operational expense and show that the payment was taken directly from your shop's **Petty Cash** fund.

• **Step 1**: Navigate to the **Expenses** or **Accounting** module.

- **Step 2**: Create a new **Expense Record**.

- **Step 3**: Select the category (e.g., **Utilities** - **Electricity**) and set the Payee to "Meralco."

- **Step 4**: Set the **Payment Mode** to **Petty Cash** and enter the amount of 9,000.

14\. **Retrieving Vehicle Service History**

**Scenario**: A customer asks for a complete record of all the work done on their car for their personal files or for resale value. You need to pull this from the **Vehicle Master List** using their **Plate Number**.

• **Step 1**: Navigate to the **Vehicle Master List** module.

- **Step 2**: Use the search bar to enter the customer's **Plate Number**.

- **Step 3**: Open the vehicle profile and click on the **Service History** tab.

- **Step 4**: Review the list of past Job Orders and click **Print** to generate the report for the customer.

15\. **Fleet Account: Statement of Account (SOA)**

**Scenario**: A **Fleet Account** customer needs to know their total unpaid balance for the month. You need to pull up their specific record and generate a **Statement of Account** that lists all outstanding invoices and the total amount due.

• **Step 1**: Navigate to the **Customer Master List** module.

- **Step 2**: Use the search bar to find the **Fleet Account** by company name.

- **Step 3**: Open the customer profile and look for the **Billing** or **Outstanding Balances** section.

- **Step 4**: Click **Print Statement of Account (SOA)** to generate the official document for their accounting department.
