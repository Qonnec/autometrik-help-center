# User Training and Orientation

**Qonnec will host a User Orientation for clients** after they avail of AutoMetrik, either through **virtual meeting or face-to-face setting**. This orientation ensures that clients are properly introduced to the system and understand how it works. In addition, the company will provide a user manual and access to an online support site at **https://autometrik.freshdesk.com/support/home**, where clients can find answers to common questions or get help if they encounter any issues by submitting a ticket.
