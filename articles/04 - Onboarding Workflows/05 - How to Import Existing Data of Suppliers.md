# How to Import Existing Data of Suppliers

Use the following procedure to **import data on your account**.

1.  **Under the Inventory** option in the left menu, click on the **Suppliers** to open the supplier management page.

2.  Look for the **Import** button at the top right of the suppliers list and **click it**. This will take you to the Import Supplier page.

3.  Ensure your data is in a **compatible format**, such as a CSV file. Include columns like Name, Code, Contact Person, Contact Number, and Date.

\[***NOTE***: If your file is not in a compatible format, download a template by clicking **Download Template** on the import page to match the required format.\]

4\. Click the **Choose File** button and browse your computer to select the prepared CSV file containing your supplier data.

5\. Click the **Preview** button to review the data that will be imported. Check for any errors or mismatches.

6\. If the preview looks correct, click **Import** **to upload the data** into the system. Wait for a confirmation message to ensure the process is successful.

7\. Return to the suppliers list by clicking **Back to List** and check that all supplier from your file are now displayed.

Use the following procedure to **export data on your account**.

1.  **Under the Inventory** option in the left menu, click on the **Suppliers** to open the supplier management page.

2.  Look for the **Export** button at the top right of the suppliers list and click it. This will download a CSV file containing all current supplier data.

3.  Choose a location on your computer to **save the downloaded file** and name it as needed.

4.  **Open the exported CSV file** using a spreadsheet program (like Excel) to verify that it includes all supplier details, such as Name, Code, Contact Person, Contact Number, and Date.

5.  **Use the exported file for backups**, sharing with other systems, or analyzing supplier information outside the AutoMetrik platform.
