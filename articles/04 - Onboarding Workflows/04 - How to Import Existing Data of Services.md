# How to Import Existing Data of Services

Use the following procedure to **import data on your account**.

1.  **Under the Services** option in the left menu, click on **Services** to open the services management page.

2.  Look for the **Import button** at the top right of the services list and click it. This will take you to the Import Services page.

3.  Ensure your data is in a compatible format, such as a **CSV** file. Include columns like Name, Code, Rate, Standard Hours, Group, and Category.

\[***NOTE***: If your file is not in a compatible format, download a template by clicking **Download Template** on the import page to match the required format.\]

4\. Click the **Choose File** button and browse your computer to select the prepared CSV file containing your service data.

5\. Click the **Preview button** to review the data that will be imported. Check for any errors or mismatches.

6\. If the preview looks correct, click **Import to upload the data into the system**. Wait for a confirmation message to ensure the process is successful.

7\. Return to the services list by clicking **Back to List** and check that all services from your file are now displayed.

Use the following procedure to **export data on your account**.

1.  Under the **Services** option in the left menu, click on Services to open the services management page.

2.  Look for the **Export button** at the top right of the services list and click it. This will download a CSV file containing all current services data.

3.  **Choose a location** on your computer to save the downloaded file and name it as needed.

4.  Open the **exported CSV file using a spreadsheet program** (like Excel) to verify that it includes all service details, such as Name, Code, Rate, Standard Hours, Group, Category, and Date.

5.  **Use the exported file for backups**, sharing with other systems, or analyzing service information outside the AutoMetrik platform.
