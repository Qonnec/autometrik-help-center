# How to Configure Print Settings in Current Branch

Use the following procedure to **customize branch’s printing**.

1.  **Access the Dashboard**: Log in to the system and navigate to the main dashboard, where key metrics like revenue, expenses, customers, inventory, and vehicles are displayed.

2.  **Locate the Select Location Dropdown**: In the upper left corner of the dashboard, find the "Select Location" button or dropdown menu (it may appear as a blue bar with the current location name).

3.  **Click to Open the Dropdown**: Click on the "Select Location" button to expand the list of available branches. This will show all branches associated with your account.

4.  **Edit Current Branch Settings**: Select the “Settings” button at the bottom left of the dropdown menu. This will take you to the three main navigations of your branch information: **Location**, **Print Settings**, and **Other Settings**.

\[***NOTE***: **Location Settings** contains information about your current branch, **Print Settings** is for your branch printable templates, and **Other Settings** is for administrative settings of your branch.\]

5\. **Scroll Through the Sections**: (Header, Estimate, Job Order, Invoice, Quick Sale, etc.). For each document type you want to customize. For Quick Sale, Payment, Deposit, Purchase Order, etc.: Keep titles simple and footers minimal (or leave blank if not used).

6\. **Save Your Edit**: Scroll to bottom, click **Save** / **Update**. Wait for confirmation.

7\. **Test Printing Templates**: Go to Vehicles or Operations and create a test Job Order / Estimate / Invoice. Click Print/Preview to check header, terms, signatures, footer appearance. Return to Print Settings to adjust if needed.
