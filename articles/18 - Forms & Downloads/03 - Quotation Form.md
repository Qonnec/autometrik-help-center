# Quotation Form

Qonnec uses this form to show the work it will do, the price, and the expected schedule for a requested change or service. Review these details before signing.

[Download the Quotation Form (PDF)](forms/Autometrik_Quotation_Form.pdf)

The quotation includes:

1. **Quotation details:** quotation number, date, valid-until date, related change request number, company or shop name, contact person, position, email address, contact number, and branch or location.

2. **Scope of work:** request title, deliverables, and anything excluded from the price or assumed in the proposal.

3. **Pricing:** description of each item, hours or quantity, rate, amount, subtotal, discount, 12% VAT, total amount due, and any pricing notes. The PDF calculates the amounts and totals from the figures entered.

4. **Timeline and payment terms:** estimated start date, estimated delivery date, and payment option.

5. **Acceptance:** names, signatures, and dates for Qonnec and the client.

The form states that work begins after the quotation is signed and the required payment is received. Changes to the agreed scope need a new or revised quotation. For a quotation for vehicle repairs inside Autometrik, see [How to Create and Convert an Estimate](#a-8-10).
