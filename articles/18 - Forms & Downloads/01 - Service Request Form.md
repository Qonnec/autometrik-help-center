# Service Request Form

Use this form to request a meeting, training, technical support, setup assistance, or a visit from Qonnec.

Use the following procedure to **request a service.**

1. [Download the Service Request Form (PDF)](forms/Autometrik_Service_Request_Form.pdf) and enter the **date, company or shop name, your name, position, email address, and contact number**.

2. Under **Type of Service**, select what you need: a meeting, training, technical support, go-live help, data setup or migration, hardware or printer setup, process review, or another service. Select the service mode: onsite, online or remote, or at the Qonnec office.

3. Enter your **preferred date and time**, an alternative date, estimated duration, complete venue address, number of attendees, onsite contact person's name and number, and branch or location.

4. Under **Purpose & Agenda**, describe what you want to accomplish. Select the modules or topics to cover and any venue requirements, such as a projector, internet connection, computers, or a training room. Add other notes if needed.

5. Enter your **name, signature, and date** under Client Confirmation. Save the completed PDF and send it to Qonnec through your usual support channel.

Fields marked with an asterisk on the form are required. For onsite services, Qonnec will confirm the schedule and send a quotation before the service takes place. Transportation or accommodation charges may be included.
