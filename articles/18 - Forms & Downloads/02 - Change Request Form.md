# Change Request Form

Use this form when you want to request a change to Autometrik, such as a new feature, a customization, or an update to a report.

Use the following procedure to **complete a change request.**

1. [Download the Change Request Form (PDF)](forms/Autometrik_Change_Request_Form.pdf) and enter the **date, your name, position, company or shop name, email address, contact number, branch or location, and desired target date**.

2. Under **Request Details**, select the application module, request type, and priority. You can choose Sales & Invoicing, Job Order / Service, Inventory, Accounting, Reports, Customer / CRM, Settings / Setup, or Other.

3. In **Description**, explain the current process or problem, what you want to change, and why the change is needed.

4. In **Business Impact**, indicate whether the change affects one branch, several branches, or all clients. Select the affected users and add any details that will help Qonnec review the request.

5. Enter your **name, signature, and date** under Client Confirmation. Save the completed PDF and send it to Qonnec through your usual support channel.

Fields marked with an asterisk on the form are required. Qonnec will review the request and provide a quotation and timeline if applicable.
