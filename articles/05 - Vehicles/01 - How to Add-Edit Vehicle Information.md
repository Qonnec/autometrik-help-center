# How to Add/Edit Vehicle Information

The AutoMetrik system makes it easy to **add and edit vehicle records**, helping you keep track of all vehicles your auto shop services. It keeps all vehicle details in one place, making it easier to manage jobs, track history, and schedule services.

Use the following procedure to **add a vehicle**.

1.  **Navigate to Vehicles**: Click on the "Vehicles" option in the left menu to open the vehicle management page.

2.  **Add a New Vehicle**: Look for a "New" or "Add Vehicle" button (usually at the top right) and click it.

3.  **Enter Vehicle Details**: Fill in the required fields, such as:
    
      - **Make**: The vehicle’s brand (e.g., Toyota).
    
      - **Model**: The specific model (e.g., Corolla).
    
      - **Year**: The vehicle’s manufacturing year.
    
      - **License Plate**: The vehicle’s plate number.
    
      - **Customer**: Link the vehicle to a customer (select from your customer list).
    
      - **VIN** (Optional): The Vehicle Identification Number, if needed.

4.  **Save the Record**: Click "Save" or "Submit" to add the vehicle to the system.

5.  **Verify**: Go back to the vehicle list to ensure the new vehicle appears.

Use the following procedure to **edit a vehicle record**.

1.  **Navigate to Vehicles**: Click on the "Vehicles" option in the left menu.

2.  **Find the Vehicle**: Scroll through the vehicle list or use the search bar to find the vehicle you want to edit.

3.  **Open the Record**: Click on the vehicle’s name or an "Edit" button next to it to open its details.

4.  **Update Details**: Make changes to fields like the customer, license plate, or other information as needed.

5.  **Save Changes**: Click "Save" or "Update" to confirm your changes.

6.  **Verify**: Check the vehicle list to ensure the updates are reflected.
