# How to Access Vehicle Notes and Reminders

Vehicle Notes are **comments or observations added by mechanics or staff** about a vehicle’s condition, repairs, or other details. These notes are visible in the vehicle’s inspection or service history. It provides a record of observations (e.g., unusual noises or wear) for future reference, improving service accuracy and communication.

Use the following procedure to **access mechanic’s notes on vehicle**.

1.  **Navigate to Vehicles:** Click on the "Vehicles" option in the left menu.

2.  **Select a Vehicle:** Find the vehicle and click it to open its details.

3.  **Access Inspections:** Click the "Inspections" tab to view or add notes.

4.  **Add a Note:** Look for the "Mechanic Notes" column, enter details (e.g., "Brakes need checking"), and save the record.

5.  **Review Notes:** Check the notes in the inspection history to see past observations.
