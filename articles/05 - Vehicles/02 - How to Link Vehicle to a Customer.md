# How to Link Vehicle to a Customer

Linking vehicles to customers means **connecting a vehicle record to the customer who owns or uses it** in the system. This ties all related information together, like service history and contact details. It helps you quickly see which customer owns a vehicle, manage their service requests, and keep accurate records for billing or communication.

Use the following procedure to **link a vehicle**.

1.  **Navigate to Vehicles:** Click on the "Vehicles" option in the left menu to open the vehicle management page.

2.  **Add or Edit a Vehicle:**
    
      - **For a New Vehicle:** Click "New" or "Add Vehicle" to create a new record.
    
      - **For an Existing Vehicle:** Find the vehicle in the list and click "Edit" to open its details.

<!-- end list -->

3.  **Select the Customer:**
    
      - In the "Customer Information" section, use the "Customer" dropdown or type-in field.
    
      - Start typing the customer’s name to search, then select the correct name from the list that appears.
    
      - If the customer isn’t listed, add them first under the "Customers" section before linking.

<!-- end list -->

4.  **Save the Link:** Click "Save Vehicle" or "Update" to save the connection between the vehicle and the customer.

5.  **Verify the Link:** Go back to the vehicle list or customer details to confirm the vehicle is now associated with the correct customer.
