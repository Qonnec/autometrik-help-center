# How to View Service History

AutoMetrik system allows you **to view service history for vehicles**, helping you track past repairs, maintenance, and costs for each customer’s car. It helps you see a vehicle’s maintenance history, identify recurring issues, and provide customers with a clear record of work completed.

Use the following procedure to **access service history of a vehicle**.

1.  **Navigate to Vehicles**: Click on the "Vehicles" option in the left menu to open the vehicle management page.

2.  **Select a Vehicle**: Find the vehicle in the list and click on it to view its details.

3.  **Access Service History**: Click "Service History" to display the full record.

4.  **Review the Details**: The service history will show:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Reference No.**: A unique identifier for each service.

  - **Services**: A list of tasks performed (e.g., Oil Top-Up, Brake Fluid Top Up) and the mechanic responsible for it.

  - **Amount**: The cost of each service.

  - **Date**: When the service was done.

  - **Status**: The current status (e.g., Released).

  - **Total**: The overall cost for all services listed.

5\. **Explore Further**: Scroll through the list to see all past services, or use filters if available to narrow down by date or status.
