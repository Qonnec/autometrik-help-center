# What is Autometrik?

**AutoMetrik** is an all-in-one management software designed specifically for **auto repair shops** and service centers. It functions as a comprehensive Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system, centralizing every aspect of your repair business—from the moment a vehicle enters the bay to the final payment.

With AutoMetrik, you can streamline your entire service workflow. You can effortlessly create professional **estimates**, convert them into active **job orders**, and generate accurate **invoices** and **payments**.

Crucially, AutoMetrik gives you full control over your stock. It allows you to **track inventory** in real-time and manage **purchase orders**, ensuring you always know exactly what parts and materials are on hand and what needs to be replenished.

AutoMetrik empowers your shop to:

  - **Master Your Financials:** Automatically generate detailed **sales reports** and **financial statements** so you always understand your shop’s profitability and cash flow.

  - **Boost Customer Retention:** Send automated **customer service reminders** for preventive maintenance, keeping your clients coming back.

  - **Streamline Daily Operations:** From tracking employee tasks to managing supplier relationships, everything is organized in one dashboard.

  - **Access Data Anywhere:** Because it’s cloud-based, you can log into AutoMetrik from any device with internet access, giving you control over your shop even when you’re away from the office.

AutoMetrik brings together everything your repair shop needs—saving you time, reducing errors, and helping your team work more efficiently.
