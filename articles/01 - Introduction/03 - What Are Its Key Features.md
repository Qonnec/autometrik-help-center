# What Are Its Key Features?

The system is a powerful and easy-to-use management platform built specifically for businesses in the automotive industry—whether you’re running a small workshop or managing a large dealership. Designed to support daily operations, AutoMetrik helps streamline key business functions like inventory control, financial tracking, customer management, and service operations.

  - **Dashboard Overview:** Provides a centralized view of business metrics, including Profit & Loss, Job Orders, Customers, Inventory, and Vehicles.

  - **Financial Management:** Tracks revenue and expenses, with breakdowns for revenue sources (Parts and Accessories, Service Income, etc.) and expenses (Bank Charges, etc.). Displays cash flow and 6-month sales trends.

  - **Inventory Tracking:** Monitors stock levels, aiding in stock management for parts and accessories.

  - **Customer and Vehicle Management:** Manages customer data and vehicle statuses supporting job order and service tracking.

  - **Reporting and Analytics:** Offers visual insights via pie charts (Revenue Breakdown) and line graphs (6-Month Sales), helping analyze financial performance over time.

  - **Operational Tools:** Includes sections for Product Inquiry, Operations, CRM, and Accounting, suggesting capabilities for customer relations, job scheduling, and financial record-keeping.

These features indicate AutoMetrik is a comprehensive auto shop management tool focused on financial oversight, inventory control, and operational efficiency.
