# Who are its Intended Users?

AutoMetrik is designed for **all types of automotive businesses**—whether you're running a small car dealership or managing a large automotive company with multiple locations. No matter the size of your business, AutoMetrik provides the tools you need to stay organized and operate efficiently.

It’s a flexible platform built specifically for the **automotive industry**, helping businesses handle their daily tasks like tracking car inventory, managing customer and supplier relationships, monitoring expenses, and coordinating employee activities.

Because it’s cloud-based, there’s no need for expensive hardware or complex setups. This makes AutoMetrik a smart choice for **small businesses that need an affordable, easy-to-use solution**, as well as **larger companies that require more advanced features and scalability**.

Whether you sell a few cars a week or manage hundreds of vehicles across multiple branches, AutoMetrik adapts to your needs and grows with your business.
