# What Are the System Requirements?

The preferred browser for using AutoMetrik is **Google Chrome**, as it offers the best performance and compatibility. However, it is also compatible with **Safari** and **Firefox**, providing flexibility across different devices and user preferences.

To streamline access, it's recommended to bookmark the login page and memorize your username on all your devices. This ensures you can log in quickly and efficiently whenever needed. In most cases, a single browser session is sufficient for daily use. However, if you need to access multiple AutoMetrik accounts simultaneously, it can be helpful to open separate user sessions or profiles in Chrome.

Use the following procedure to **manage multiple logins**.

1.  Open Chrome. At the top right, click the button with your name or People.

2.  Click Manage people then Add Person.

3.  Choose a photo and a name.

4.  Click Save. A new window will open and ask you to sign in

5.  Sign into Chrome with a Google Account for the new profile
