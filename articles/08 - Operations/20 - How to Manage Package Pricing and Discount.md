# How to Manage Package Pricing and Discount

Use the following procedure to **add a new package and set the pricing.**

1.  **Access the Packages Section**: Navigate to the "Administration" menu on the left sidebar, expand it, and click on "Packages".

2.  **Create a New Package**: Click the "New" button in the top right corner to open the "New Package" form.

3.  Enter Package Details:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Name**: Enter the package name in the "Name" field.

  - **Code**: Enter the package code in the "Code" field.

  - **Line Items Printout Options**:

  - **Hide Services**: Check "No" unless services should be hidden on printouts.

  - **Hide Parts & Materials**: Check "No" unless parts and materials should be hidden on printouts.

  - **Display Code**: Check "No" unless the code should be displayed on printouts.

  - **Hide Amounts**: Check "No" unless amounts should be hidden on printouts.

  - **Replacement Text**: Leave blank or enter replacement text if applicable.

4\. Add Labor:

  - **Labor**: Type or select the service name from the dropdown.

  - **Rate, Hours, Amount**: Enter the rate, hours, and amount; these will calculate automatically based on the service.

  - **Add Service**: Click "+ Add Service" to include additional services if needed.

5\. Add Parts & Materials:

  - **Category**: Select or type the category from the dropdown.

  - **Parts & Materials**: Type in the product name or part number and select from the dropdown.

  - **Part No**: Enter the part number.

  - **Price**: Input the price.

  - **Quantity**: Specify the quantity.

  - **Amount**: The amount will calculate automatically.

  - **Add Product**: Click "+ Add Product" to include additional parts or materials.

6\. **Configure Pricing and Discounts**:

  - The pricing and discounts are reflected in the "Rate," "Price," and "Amount" fields for labor and parts, respectively. Adjust these values to set the desired pricing structure.

  - Discounts can be applied by modifying the "Amount" fields or through package-specific discount settings if available in the system.

7\. **Review Summary**:

  - Use the "Summary" section to add any remarks.

  - Check the "Actual Subtotal," "VAT (12.0000%)," and "Total Amount" for accuracy.

8\. **Save the Package**: Click "Create Package" to save the new package.

Use the following procedure to **edit the pricing of an existing package.**

1.  **Access the Packages Section**: Navigate to the "Administration" menu on the left sidebar, expand it, and click on "Packages".

2.  **Select the Package**: Locate and click on the existing package you want to edit from the list to open its details (e.g., "Package Info" as shown).

3.  **Modify Labor Price**:

<!-- end list -->

  - Under the "Labor" section, find the service entry you wish to adjust.

  - Update the "Rate" field with the new price.

  - The "Amount" field will automatically recalculate based on the updated rate and hours.

4\. **Modify Parts & Materials Price**:

  - Under the "Parts & Materials" section, find the product entry you wish to adjust.

  - Update the "Price" field with the new price.

  - The "Amount" field will automatically recalculate based on the updated price and quantity.

5\. **Review Changes:**

  - Check the "Summary" section to ensure the "Actual Subtotal," "VAT (12.00%)," and "Total Amount" reflect the updated prices.

  - Add any remarks in the "Summary" section if needed.

6\. **Save the Changes**: Click the appropriate save or update button (typically available in the interface after editing) to apply the new prices.
