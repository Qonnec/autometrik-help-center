# The Difference of Queued, In Progress, and Completed

These refer to **distinct stages of job status monitoring on the Jobs Board**, reflecting the lifecycle of a service task. These statuses are crucial for managing workflow, ensuring timely service delivery, and maintaining accurate records for billing and performance evaluation.

| **Name**    | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Queued      | Represents jobs that have been scheduled or assigned but have not yet started, awaiting initiation by a mechanic—though not explicitly shown in the provided image, it typically precedes the "OPEN" state where jobs are ready for action. |
| In Progress | Indicates jobs currently being worked on by a mechanic, with active clock-in/clock-out tracking to monitor time spent.                                                                                                                      |
| Completed   | Signifies jobs that have been fully executed, with all tasks finished and time logged, ready for review or invoicing.                                                                                                                       |
