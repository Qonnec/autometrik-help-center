# How to Clock-In/Clock-Out Per Job

Use the following procedure to **start time tracking per job.**

1.  **Access the Jobs Board**: Navigate to the "Operations" menu on the left sidebar and click on "Jobs Board".

2.  **Select a Job**: In the "OPEN" section, locate the job you want to manage (e.g., "Clean Air Filter" assigned to Alfred Mendoza).

3.  **Clock-In**:

<!-- end list -->

  - Click the job to open the "Clock Entries" popup.

  - Click the "Clock-In" button to start tracking time for the selected job.

  - The system will record the clock-in time (e.g., 08:35 PM).

4\. **Clock-Out**:

  - Once the task is complete, click the "Clock-Out" button in the "Clock Entries" popup.

  - Select a reason for clocking out (e.g., "Completed," "Lunch Break," "Missing Parts") from the dropdown.

  - The system will record the clock-out time (e.g., 08:35 PM) and calculate the total hours worked.

5\. **Review and Close**:

  - Verify the clock-in and clock-out times and the total hours (e.g., 1.00 HR) in the popup.

  - Click "Close" to exit the "Clock Entries" popup.
