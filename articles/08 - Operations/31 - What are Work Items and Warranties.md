# What are Work Items and Warranties

**Work items refer to the specific tasks or services performed on a vehicle, such as repairs, maintenance, or part replacements**, which are tracked and managed through the system. These items are detailed in job orders, quick sales, or appointments and include specifics like part numbers, labor hours, and costs. **Warranties**, on the other hand, are **guarantees provided by the shop or parts manufacturers, ensuring that the work or parts will perform as expected for a specified period or mileage.**
