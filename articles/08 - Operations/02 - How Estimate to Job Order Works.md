# How Estimate to Job Order Works

| **Name**  | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Estimate  | Accessed under the Operations menu on the left-hand side, the Estimate feature **allows users to create detailed cost projections for vehicle repairs or services**. Users input customer details, vehicle information, and a list of required services or parts. The system calculates an estimated cost, which is then presented to the customer for approval. This step serves as the initial planning phase, helping to set expectations and secure customer consent before proceeding. |
| Job Order | Once an estimate is approved, it transitions into a Job Order under the same Operations menu. This process **involves converting the estimate into an actionable work order, assigning it to a technician, and scheduling the task**. The dashboard displays active job orders, allowing real-time tracking of status, progress, and completion.                                                                                                                                            |

1.  **Creating an Estimate**:

<!-- end list -->

  - You start by filling out the "New Estimate" form with details such as customer name, vehicle information, service advisor, mileage, and estimated days.

  - You add labor services (e.g., "Temperature Sending Unit Replacement" and "AC High Pressure Hose Replacement") with their respective rates, hours, and amounts.

  - Optionally, you can add parts and materials with part numbers, prices, quantities, and amounts.

  - The system calculates the total estimated cost based on the entered data.

2\. **Saving the Estimate**:

  - Once saved (as indicated by the "Save Successful" message), the estimate is stored with a reference number (e.g., EST0001238) and includes the date, expiry date, and other details.

3\. **Converting to a Job Order**:

  - The system allows you to create a new job order based on the saved estimate by clicking the “Convert to Job Order” in the Estimate details.

  - You may assign mechanics to each labor task and add notes or additional details.

  - The system updates the status (e.g., "Open") and generates a new reference number (e.g., RO0001348).

4\. **Processing the Job Order**:

  - After creating the job order, you can further modify it by adding more services, parts, or notes. The "Job Order / Details" view shows the saved information, including labor amounts, parts (e.g., "145/65R15 72S DUNLOP SPOR T2030"), and a summary with subtotals, VAT, and net amounts.

  - The job order can then be tracked through different statuses (e.g., In-Process, Completed, Released) and used to create purchase requests or deposits if needed.

In summary, **creating an estimate sets up a preliminary cost breakdown**, which **can** then **be converted** into a **job order to initiate and manage the actual repair process**, carrying over relevant details for efficiency.
