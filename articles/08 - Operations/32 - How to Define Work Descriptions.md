# How to Define Work Descriptions

Use the following procedure to **access job description.**

1.  **Access Job-Related Sections**: Navigate to the "Operations" menu on the left sidebar, which contains sections like "Job Order," "Quick Sale," or "Jobs Board" where work items are typically managed.

2.  **Open a Specific Job or Order**:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Job Order**: Click "Job Order" and select an existing job to view associated work items, such as labor tasks or parts used.

  - **Quick Sale**: Click "Quick Sale" and open a sale to see work items listed under "Parts & Materials."

  - **Jobs Board**: Click "Jobs Board" and check the "OPEN," "IN-PROGRESS," or "COMPLETED" tabs to view work items assigned to mechanics.

3\. **Review Details**: Within these sections, look for fields or tabs detailing tasks, parts, or services (e.g., "Parts & Materials" or task descriptions) to locate the specific work items.
