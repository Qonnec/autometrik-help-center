# What are Billing and Payments

Billing refers to the process of generating and issuing an invoice to a customer for goods or services provided, detailing the amount owed and payment terms. Payment is the action of settling the invoiced amount by the customer, which can be made through various methods like cash, bank transfer, or online platforms, depending on the system's options. In the AutoMetrik system, **billing involves converting an estimate into an invoice**, while **payment is processed after the customer receives and approves the invoice**.
