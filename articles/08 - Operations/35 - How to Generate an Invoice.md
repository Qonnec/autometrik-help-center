# How to Generate an Invoice

Use the following procedure to **create an invoice.**

1.  **Access the Operations Section**:

<!-- end list -->

  - From the left sidebar, click on "Operations" to expand the menu, then select an operation that you need to make an invoice at to view the list.

2\. **Locate the Desired Data**:

  - For example, in the "Estimates" table, find the estimate you want to convert into an invoice.

  - Check the "Status" column to ensure the estimate is marked as "Converted."

3\. **View Estimate Details**: Click on the estimate number to open its details. This will display the estimate summary, including customer information, vehicle details, labor, and amount.

4**. Print or Convert the Estimate**

  - Look for a "Print" option or icon in the "Action" column of the Estimates table.

  - Click "Print" to generate a printable version of the estimate.

5\. **Verify and Adjust Invoice Details**

6\. **Review the printed estimate for accuracy**: If adjustments are needed, edit the details in the estimate before conversion, if the system permits.

7**. Save and Finalize**: Save the invoice in the system.
