# How to Manage Deposits and Overpayments

Use the following procedure to **manage deposits and overpayments.**

1.  Navigate to the main dashboard, click **Operations** in the left-hand menu, then select **Deposits**.

2.  **View a list of recorded deposits on the Deposits page**, including details such as deposit number, customer name, job order, payment method, amount, used amount, refunded amount, date, and action options.

3.  **Click the deposit number** or the action icon to view deposit details.

4.  **Review deposit information**, including customer, job order number, payment method, deposit amount, used amount, balance, and any associated description.

5.  **If there is an overpayment or unused balance**, click the "**Refund**" button to initiate a refund process.

6.  **Enter refund details**, including refund amount, date of refund, and reason for refund, then click Refund to process the transaction.

7.  Us**e the Search field at the top or apply filters to find a specific deposit** by customer name, job order number, or date.
