# How to View Technician Performance

Use the following procedure to **monitor technician performance.**

1.  **Access the Jobs Board**: Navigate to the "Operations" menu on the left sidebar and click on "Jobs Board".

2.  **View Job Status Categories**: Observe the tabs labeled "OPEN," "IN-PROGRESS," and "COMPLETED" to see the current status of jobs.

<!-- end list -->

  - **OPEN**: Displays jobs awaiting assignment or start.

  - **IN-PROGRESS**: Shows jobs currently being worked on.

  - **COMPLETED**: Lists jobs that have been finished.

3\. **Check Individual Job Details**:

  - Click on a specific job to view details such as the assigned mechanic, vehicle, and estimated hours.

  - For in-progress jobs, check the clock-in/out times and current status updates (e.g., "Clocked-In" at 08:35 PM).

  - For completed jobs, review the total hours worked and completion time.

4\. **Apply Filters** **(Optional):** Use the "Select open job orders" dropdown and "Apply Filter" button to focus on specific jobs or mechanics.

5\. **Monitor Progress**: Regularly check the status transitions to track job progression and ensure timely completion. Note that with every status change (e.g., from OPEN to IN-PROGRESS or IN-PROGRESS to COMPLETED), the platform automatically sends an SMS to the customer as an update or confirmation of the job status.
