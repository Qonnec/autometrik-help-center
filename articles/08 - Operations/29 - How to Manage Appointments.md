# How to Manage Appointments

Appointments in the AutoMetrik system refer to **scheduled time slots where customers book services or consultations for their vehicles**. This allows the shop to organize customer visits, assign mechanics or service advisors, and link to specific job orders or quick sales, ensuring efficient workflow and resource allocation. Appointments include details such as customer information, vehicle data, preferred date and time, and service requests, enabling the shop to plan ahead, reduce wait times, and enhance customer satisfaction by providing a structured approach to service delivery.

Use the following procedure to **manage appointments.**

1.  **Access Appointments**: Navigate to the "Operations" menu on the left sidebar and click on "Appointments".

2.  **View Appointment List**: The interface will display a list of all scheduled appointments, showing details such as customer names, vehicle information, appointment dates and times, and assigned mechanics or service advisors.

3.  **Filter or Search (Optional)**: Use any available filter or search options (e.g., date range or customer name) to narrow down the list if the interface provides such functionality.

4.  **Select an Appointment**: Click on a specific appointment to view detailed information, including status, notes, or linked job orders.
