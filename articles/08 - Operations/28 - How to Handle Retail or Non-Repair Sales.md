# How to Handle Retail or Non-Repair Sales

Use the following procedure to **create a retail sale.**

1.  **Access Quick Sale**: Navigate to the "Operations" menu on the left sidebar and click on "Quick Sale".

2.  **Create a New Quick Sale**: Click the "New" button in the top right corner to open the "New Quick Sale" form.

3.  **Enter Basic Information**:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Reference No**: Leave as "System Generated" or enter a custom reference if needed.

  - **Date**: Set to the current date (e.g., 20 Jun 2025).

  - **Status**: Select "New" (or the appropriate status).

  - **Customer**: Type or select the customer name from the dropdown.

  - **Sales** Person: Select the sales person from the dropdown.

  - **Payment Method**: Select the payment method from the dropdown.

  - Payment Reference: Enter a payment reference number if applicable.

4\. **Add Parts &** **Materials**:

  - **Parts & Materials**: Type in the product name or part number and select from the dropdown.

  - **Part No**: Enter the part number.

  - **Price**: Input the price.

  - **Quantity**: Specify the quantity.

  - **Amount**: The amount will calculate automatically.

  - **Add Product**: Click "+ Add Product" to include additional items.

5**. Review Summary**:

  - Use the "Summary" section to add any remarks.

  - Check the "Subtotal," "Discount," "VAT incl. (12.0000%)," "Total Amount," "Payment," and "Change" for accuracy.

6\. **Save the Sale**: Click "Create Quick Sale" to save as a draft or "Create & Complete" to finalize the transaction.

This process enables efficient management of retail or non-repair sales, such as selling parts or accessories, by providing a streamlined, immediate transaction option that enhances customer convenience and diversifies revenue streams.

Use the following procedure to **edit an existing retail sale.**

1.  **Access Quick Sale Details**: Navigate to the "Operations" menu on the left sidebar and click on "Quick Sale". Locate and click on the existing quick sale to open its details.

2.  **Modify Basic Information**:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Reference** **No**: View the system-generated reference number, which cannot be edited.

  - **Date**: Update the date if needed.

  - **Status**: Adjust the status if applicable.

  - **Customer**: Edit the customer name by typing or selecting a new name from the dropdown.

  - **Sales Person**: Select a different sales person from the dropdown if necessary.

  - **Payment Method**: Update the payment method and enter a new payment reference if required.

3**. Edit Parts & Materials**:

  - Under "Parts & Materials," locate the existing item.

  - Modify the "Part No," "Price," "Quantity," or "Amount" fields as needed.

  - Click "+ Add Product" to include additional items or remove existing ones if supported by the interface.

4\. **Review Summary**:

  - Update any remarks in the "Summary" section.

  - Verify the "Subtotal," "Discount," "VAT incl. (12.0000%)," "Total Amount," "Payment," and "Change" to reflect the changes.

5**. Save Changes**: Click "Save Quick Sale" to apply the edits.
