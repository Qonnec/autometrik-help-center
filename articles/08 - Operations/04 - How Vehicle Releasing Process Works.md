# How Vehicle Releasing Process Works

1.  **Complete Job Order**:

<!-- end list -->

  - Ensure all services and tasks listed in the Job Order (e.g., repairs, maintenance like Change Oil) are fully completed and documented.

  - Verify that all parts, labor, and additional notes (e.g., specific repairs or observations) are updated in the Job Order details.

2\. **Review Invoice and Payment**:

  - Confirm that the invoice associated with the Job Order has been created, finalized, and marked as paid in the system.

  - Ensure payment details (e.g., Payment Amount, Payment Method, Reference No) are recorded and the invoice balance is 0.00.)

3\. **Prepare for Vehicle Release**:

  - Access the Job Order in the system and navigate to the "Release Vehicle" or "Close Job Order" option.

  - Update the Job Order status to "Released" to indicate completion of the repair process.

4\. **Record Release Details**:

  - Enter the following information in the system:

<!-- end list -->

1.  Released By: Name of the staff authorizing the release (e.g., Jane Flores).

2.  Release Time: Date and time of release (e.g., 4:15 PM, 2025-07-21).

3.  Remarks: Note any relevant details (e.g., "Released to owner").

<!-- end list -->

  - Verify that all fields are correctly filled out.

5\. **Close the Job Order**:

  - Save the release details and confirm the Job Order closure in the system.

  - Ensure the system updates the Job Order status to "Closed" and logs the release details.

6\. **Notify the Customer**:

  - Inform the customer that the vehicle is ready for pickup, either through direct communication or via the system’s notification channel (e.g., SMS, Email, In-app message).

  - Provide any final instructions, such as pickup location or additional care tips for the vehicle.

7\. **Automatic System Updates**:

  - The system automatically logs the release details and any generated Service Reminders in the appropriate sections (e.g., Job Order history, Service Reminder logs).

  - If applicable, ensure journal entries for the Job Order and payment are reflected in the "Accounting" section for accurate financial tracking.

In summary, **the vehicle release process finalizes the Job Order by confirming service completion, payment, and release details**, while generating Service Reminders to ensure future maintenance is scheduled efficiently.
