# How to Perform Inspection

Use the following procedure to **access inspection list.**

1.  Click **Operations** on the left-hand menu to expand the options.

2.  Select **Inspection** from the submenu to open the vehicle inspections page.

3.  The **inspection list will display automatically**, showing columns such as Reference, Customer, Contact No, Vehicle, Plate No, Mechanic, Date, and Action.

4.  Use the **Search** bar at the top right to filter the list by entering a specific reference number, customer name, or other details.

5.  **Click the Filter button** (if available) to apply additional criteria, such as date range or mechanic, to refine the list.

6.  **Scroll through the list** or use the page navigation (if multiple pages) to review all inspection records.

7.  Click on any entry in **the Action column** to access detailed information about a specific inspection.
