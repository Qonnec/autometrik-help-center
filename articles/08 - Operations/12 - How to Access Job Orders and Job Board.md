# How to Access Job Orders and Job Board

A job order in AutoMetrik platform is a **document or record that outlines the specific tasks, services, or repairs to be performed on a vehicle**, based on an approved estimate. It includes details such as the customer information, vehicle details, assigned technician, labor and parts required, and the timeline for completion. Once an estimate is created and approved, a job order is generated to initiate and track the actual work process in the auto shop.

Use the following procedure to **access the job order list.**

1.  From the left sidebar, click on "**Operations**."

2.  Select "**Job Order**" from the dropdown menu.

3.  The job order page will **display a list of existing job orders**, where you can view, create, or manage them.

Use the following procedure to **edit a job order.**

1.  From the left sidebar, click on "**Operations**."

2.  Select "**Job Order**" from the dropdown menu.

3.  **Locate the job order you want to edit** from the list.

4.  **Click on the Reference Number** of the desired job order.

5.  The job order details will open, **allowing you to modify fields** such as Date, Promised Date, Status, Customer, Vehicle, Service Advisor, Mileage, Customer Source Channel, Customer PO, Labor, Mechanic, Next Service, Rate, Hours, Amount, and notes.

6.  **Make the necessary changes**.

7.  Save the updated job order **by clicking the appropriate save** or update button.
