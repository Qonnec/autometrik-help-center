# How to Select Customer/Vehicle in Creating Job Order

Use the following procedure to **select a customer and vehicle in creating a job order.**

1.  **Navigation**: From the main dashboard, click on Service in the left-hand menu, then select Job Orders.

2.  **Create New Job Order**: Click the New button in the top right corner to start a new job order.

3.  **Select Customer**: In the job order creation form, use the Customer field (e.g., search bar or dropdown) to enter the customer’s name or ID. Start typing to search, then select the correct customer from the list. If the customer is new, choose the option to add them by entering their details.

4.  **Select Vehicle**: In the Vehicle section, either select an existing vehicle linked to the customer from the dropdown or click Add Vehicle to input new vehicle details (e.g., make, model, year, VIN).

5.  **Verify Details**: Review the selected customer and vehicle information to ensure accuracy, including contact details and vehicle specifics.

6.  **Proceed**: Once the customer and vehicle are selected, click Next or Save to continue with additional job order details or finalize the entry.
