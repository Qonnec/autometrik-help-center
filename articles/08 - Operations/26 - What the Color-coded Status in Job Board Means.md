# What the Color-coded Status in Job Board Means

AutoMetrik utilizes color-coded statuses on the Jobs Board to visually distinguish the progress of jobs, enhancing efficiency in monitoring. The statuses are represented as follows:

| **Name**      | **Color** | **Description**                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Queued / Open | Orange    | Indicates jobs that are scheduled or assigned but not yet started, signaling that these tasks are awaiting initiation. |
| In Progress   | Blue      | Denotes jobs currently being worked on, highlighting active tasks with ongoing time tracking.                          |
| Completed     | Green     | Marks jobs that have been fully executed, confirming task completion and readiness for review or invoicing.            |

These color-coded statuses allow mechanics and managers to quickly assess the status of jobs at a glance, facilitating better workflow management and ensuring timely updates across the shop's operations.
