# What are Packages

AutoMetrik offers **predefined bundles of services** or products that can be offered to customers to streamline job orders and enhance efficiency. These **packages combine related labor tasks, parts, or maintenance procedures into a single offering, allowing for quicker selection and application during the job order creation process**. They are implemented to simplify the workflow for service advisors and mechanics by reducing the need to manually input individual services or products, ensuring consistency in pricing and service delivery, and providing customers with clear, cost-effective options. Additionally, packages facilitate better inventory management and tracking, as they can be tied to specific parts and quantities, while also enabling the application of discounts or incentives to encourage sales, ultimately improving customer satisfaction and operational productivity.
