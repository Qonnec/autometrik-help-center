# How to Use Inspection Templates

Use the following procedure to **use the exterior template.**

1.  **Navigate to the Exterior section** of the inspection form.

2.  **Click on the vehicle diagram template** displayed, which shows multiple views (front, rear, side, top, interior).

3.  **Use the drawing tools** provided to mark specific areas of concern directly on the diagram (e.g., draw circles or lines around damaged parts).

4.  **Add labels by clicking** on the marked areas and typing descriptions (e.g., "Scratch on hood" or "Dented bumper") to document issues visually.

5.  **Save the annotated diagram** as part of the inspection record.
