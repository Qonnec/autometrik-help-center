# How Does Customer Workflow Work?

The customer workflow in AutoMetrik **manages the entire customer journey**, from inquiry to job completion and payment, ensuring efficient service delivery and accurate record-keeping. This process integrates with inventory, operations, and accounting modules to provide a cohesive experience, leveraging the FIFO inventory method for parts allocation and cost tracking.

The customer workflow in AutoMetrik involves:

  - **Inquiry and Estimate**: Capture customer needs and provide cost estimates.

  - **Job Order Creation**: Schedule and allocate parts using FIFO inventory.

  - **Service Execution**: Perform repairs, consuming parts and logging labor.

  - **Invoicing**: Bill customers, recognizing revenue and FIFO-based COGS.

  - **Payment**: Collect payment and clear receivables.

  - **Follow-Up**: Update customer records and schedule future services.
