# How to Access Estimate

This refers to a detailed assessment or quotation provided to a customer for vehicle-related services or repairs. It typically includes information such as the customer's name, contact number, vehicle details (e.g., make, model, and plate number), the estimated cost (amount), job order number, date of the estimate, and its status (e.g., completed). This helps auto shops plan and communicate the expected costs and scope of work to customers.

Use the following procedure to **access estimates list.**

1.  From the left sidebar, click on "**Operations**."

2.  Select "**Estimate**" from the dropdown menu.

3.  You will see a table listing all estimates, including details like Customer, Contact Number, Vehicle, Plate Number, Amount, Job Order Number, Date, and Status.
