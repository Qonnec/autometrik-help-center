# How Service Reminders and Follow-Up Works

Accessed under the Operations menu on the left-hand side, the Optional Follow-ups feature includes **Service Reminders and Warranty options to maintain customer relationships and ensure vehicle upkeep.** For Service Reminders, users can set a future date to notify customers of recommended maintenance, accessible via the Appointments or Vehicles section, helping to schedule timely follow-up services and enhance vehicle performance. For Warranty, users can link warranty details to completed job orders under the Warranties option, tracking coverage periods and notifying customers of warranty expirations or claims. These features, integrated with the dashboard’s revenue and expense data, encourage repeat business and ensure compliance with service agreements.

1.  **Verify Service Reminder Settings**:

<!-- end list -->

  - Ensure Service Reminders are enabled in the system settings to allow automatic generation of reminders for services with defined Next Service Dates (e.g., Change Oil due +3 months from service date).

  - Confirm the preferred notification channels (e.g., SMS, Email, In-app) are configured for customer communication.

2\. **Review Generated Service Reminders**:

  - Access the "Service Reminders" section in the system, typically found in the Operations menu or a dedicated reminders tab.

  - Check the list of automatically generated reminders, which include:
    
    1.  Customer name and contact details.
    
    2.  Vehicle details (make, model, plate number).
    
    3.  Service type (e.g., Change Oil) and target due date (e.g., 2025-10-21 for a service completed on 2025-07-21).
    
    4.  Notification channel selected.

3\. **Send Initial Reminder Notifications**:

  - Trigger the system to send reminders to customers via the configured notification channel.

  - Example SMS Notification:  
    *Hi John Dela Cruz, your Toyota Vios (ABC-1234) is due for a Change Oil service on 2025-10-21. Please book your appointment at ABC Auto Repair. Call 0917-123-4567. Thank you\!*

  - Ensure the notification is sent at the appropriate time (e.g., one week before the due date or as per system settings).

4\. **Track Customer Responses**:

  - Monitor the "Service Reminders" section for customer replies or booking confirmations (e.g., through In-app responses or direct calls).

  - Update the reminder status in the system (e.g., "Acknowledged," "Booking Scheduled," or "No Response").

5\. **Follow Up on Unresponsive Customers**:

  - If no response is received within a set period (e.g., 3 days after the initial reminder), send a follow-up notification via the same or an alternative channel (e.g., Email if SMS was used initially).

  - Example Follow-Up Email:  
    *Dear John Dela Cruz, this is a reminder that your Toyota Vios (ABC-1234) is due for a Change Oil service on 2025-10-21. Please schedule your appointment by contacting ABC Auto Repair at 0917-123-4567 or through our app. Thank you\!*

  - Log the follow-up attempt in the system.

6\. **Assist with Appointment Scheduling**:

  - If the customer responds and requests an appointment, assist in scheduling the service through the system or by coordinating with the service advisor.

  - Create a new estimate or job order linked to the reminder, carrying over relevant details (e.g., customer, vehicle, and service type).

7\. **Update Reminder Status**:

  - Once the customer books an appointment or the service is completed, update the reminder status to "Completed" or "Scheduled" in the system.

  - If the customer declines the service, mark the reminder as "Declined" and add any relevant remarks (e.g., "Customer postponed service").

8\. **Log Follow-Up Activities**:

  - Ensure all follow-up actions (e.g., notifications sent, customer responses, or appointment scheduling) are logged in the system under the respective Service Reminder entry.

  - Verify that the system automatically updates related records (e.g., customer history or upcoming job orders) for accurate tracking.

In summary, the **service reminder follow-up process ensures timely customer communication for upcoming maintenance, tracks responses, and facilitates appointment scheduling** to maintain vehicle care efficiency.
