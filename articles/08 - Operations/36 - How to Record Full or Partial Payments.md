# How to Record Full or Partial Payments

Use the following procedure to **manage payments.**

1.  Navigate to the main dashboard, click **Operations** in the left-hand menu, then select **Payments**.

2.  **View a list of recorded payments** on the Payments page, including details such as payment date, amount, method, and associated job order or invoice.

3.  **Click the New Payment** button, typically located in the top right corner, to add a new payment.

4.  **Enter payment details** such as the **selected related job order** or invoice from the dropdown or search field, the **payment type** (e.g., full or partial), **amount paid**, and the **payment method** (e.g., cash, credit card, check).

5.  **Add the transaction date** and any **reference number** if applicable.

6.  **Review the details**, then click **Save** or **Apply Payment** to record the transaction.

7.  **Use the Search field** at the top to find a specific payment by date, amount, or job order number.
