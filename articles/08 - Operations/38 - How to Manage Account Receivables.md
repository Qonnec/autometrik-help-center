# How to Manage Account Receivables

Use the following procedure to **manage accounts receivables.**

1.  Navigate to the main dashboard, click **Operations** in the left-hand menu, then select **Accounts Receivable**.

2.  **View a list of customer receivables** on the Receivables page, including details such as customer name, invoices, aging periods, current balance, and payment options.

3.  **Click the "Pay Now" button next to a customer’s balance**, typically located in the Action column, to process a new payment.

4.  **Enter payment details** such as the selected customer from the displayed row, the payment amount, payment date, and payment method.

5.  **Add a payment reference number** if applicable and any remarks as needed.

6.  **Review the details**, then click Create Payment or Create & Complete to record the transaction.

7.  **Use the Search field at the top to find a specific receivable** by customer name, invoice number, or balance amount.
