# How to Attach Notes for Diagnostic Results

Use the following procedure to **add notes.**

1.  Add **Remarks** (Notes):
    
      - Scroll to the "**Remarks**" section.
    
      - Click inside the text box under "**Use this section to write your remarks**."
    
      - **Type your notes** or comments as needed.

2.  Attach **Diagnostic Results** (Text Files):

<!-- end list -->

  - Scroll to the "**Diagnostic Results**" section.

  - Click the "**Select File**" button to upload a file.

  - Browse your device, select the desired file, and click "**Open**" to import it.

  - Alternatively, **you can manually enter diagnostic results** in the text box if preferred.
