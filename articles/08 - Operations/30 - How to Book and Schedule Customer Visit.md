# How to Book and Schedule Customer Visit

Use the following procedure to **book an appointment.**

1.  **Access Appointments**: Navigate to the "Operations" menu on the left sidebar and click on "Appointments".

2.  **Add New Appointment**: Click the "+ Add new appointment" button (typically located in the top right corner) to open the appointment creation form.

3.  **Enter Customer Information**:

<!-- end list -->

  - **Customer**: Type or select the customer name from the dropdown.

  - **Mobile No**: Enter the customer’s mobile number.

  - **Email Address**: Input the customer’s email address.

  - **Set Appointment Details**:

  - **Schedule**: Select the date (e.g., 20 Jun 2025) using the calendar.

  - **Appointment Time**: Choose the time (e.g., 8:00 AM) from the time picker.

  - **Appointment Type**: Select the type (e.g., Maintenance) from the dropdown or type a new type.

  - **Description**: Add any relevant notes or details in the description field.

4\. **Save the Appointment**: Click the "Save changes" button to confirm and book the appointment.

5\. **Close the Form**: Click "Close" to exit the form after saving.
