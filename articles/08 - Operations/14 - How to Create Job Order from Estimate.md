# How to Create Job Order from Estimate

Use the following procedure to **create a job order from estimates.**

1.  **Navigation**: From the main dashboard, click on Service in the left-hand menu, then select Estimates.

2.  **Select Estimate**: Locate the estimate you want to convert into a job order by using the search bar or scrolling through the list, then click on it to open.

3.  **Create Job Order**: In the estimate details view, click the Create Job Order button (usually located at the top or bottom of the page).

4.  **Review Details**: Verify the transferred information, such as customer details, vehicle information, and line items, and make any necessary adjustments.

5.  **Assign Technician**: Select a technician or team from the dropdown menu to assign the job order, if required.

6.  **Add Notes**: Include any additional instructions or notes in the provided text field, if needed.

7.  **Save Job Order**: Click the Save or Create button to finalize the job order. The system will generate it based on the estimate and move it to the job order queue.

8.  **Confirm**: Check the job order status or preview it to ensure all details are correct.
