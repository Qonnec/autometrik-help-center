# How to Manage Supplier Prices

Use the following procedure to **add or view supplier prices.**

1.  Click **Inventory** on the menu.

2.  Select **Products** from the submenu.

3.  **Choose your selected product** from the product list.

4.  **Navigate to the Suppliers** tab (next to Serial Numbers).

5.  **View or enter supplier details**, including purchase costs, which represent the supplier price.

6.  **Update the Purchase Cost** under the Price Information section if supplier prices change.

7.  **Save changes** by clicking "Save Product."
