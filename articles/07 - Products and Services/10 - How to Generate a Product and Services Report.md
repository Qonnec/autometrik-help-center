# How to Generate a Product and Services Report

Accessible through the Reports menu, this tool compiles detailed information that helps **track various aspects of the business**, including stock management and service performance. They also play a critical role in financial oversight by offering insights into revenue streams, which supports effective budgeting and financial planning. Additionally, these reports enable performance analysis, **allowing businesses to evaluate the popularity and efficiency of their offerings to make informed adjustments.**

Use the following procedure to **generate a Sales by Product report.**

1.  Click **Reports** on the left-hand menu.

2.  Select **Sales by Product** from the submenu.

3.  In the Filter section:

<!-- end list -->

  - Enter a Product name or leave blank to include all products.

  - Enter a Product Group name or leave blank.

  - Enter a Category name or leave blank.

  - Enter a Manufacturer name or leave blank.

  - Set the From date (e.g., 1 Jun 2025) using the calendar dropdown.

  - Set the To date (e.g., 12 Jun 2025) using the calendar dropdown.

4**. Click Generate Report** to create the report.

5**. Review the generated report** for sales data within the specified criteria.

6\. Click **Export** to save the report in a compatible format.

Use the following procedure to **generate a Vehicle Transaction report.**

1.  Click **Reports** on the left-hand menu.

2.  Select **Vehicle Transactions** Report from the submenu.

3.  In the Filter section:

<!-- end list -->

  - Enter a Customer name or leave blank to include all customers.

  - Enter a Plate No or leave blank.

  - Set the From date (e.g., 1 Jan 2025) using the calendar dropdown.

  - Set the To date (e.g., 12 Jun 2025) using the calendar dropdown.

4**. Click Generate Report** to create the report.

5**. Review the generated report** for transaction details, including job number, customer info, vehicle details, and visit dates.

6\. Click **Export** to save the report in a compatible format.
