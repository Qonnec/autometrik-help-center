# How to Perform Product Maintenance

Product maintenance refers to the process of regularly updating, managing, and optimizing product information and related data within an inventory or management system. This includes adjusting details such as product names, part numbers, pricing, stock levels, supplier information, unit conversions, vehicle associations, and transaction records to ensure accuracy and relevance.

**Why is it Important?**

  - **Accuracy**: Keeps product data current, reducing errors in orders, pricing, and inventory tracking.

  - **Efficiency**: Streamlines operations by ensuring all teams (e.g., sales, procurement) work with the latest information.

  - **Customer Satisfaction**: Provides accurate product details to customers, enhancing trust and service quality.

  - **Cost Management**: Helps monitor costs and adjust pricing or stock levels to maintain profitability.

  - **Compliance**: Ensures adherence to inventory and regulatory requirements through updated records.

Use the following procedure to **check products**.

1.  **Navigate to the Products Section**: On the left sidebar, locate and click on the "Inventory" menu to expand it. Then, click on "Products" under Inventory. This will display the main Products list page.

2.  **Select the Product to Maintain**: In the Products list, use the search bar at the top right to find the specific product. Click on the product's name in the table to open its detailed view.

3.  **Update Product Information (Product Info Tab):**

<!-- end list -->

  - The page defaults to the "Product Info" tab.

  - Edit fields in the "Product Information" section, such as Name, Part No, Product Group, Print Display Name, Category, Unit of Measure, Description, Manufacturer, Manufacturer No, Color, Expiry Date, Serialized, Display Notes, and Incentive.

  - Scroll down to the "Price Information" section and update Purchase Cost, Product Pricing, Markup Rate, and Sell Price.

  - In the "Inventory Details" section, toggle "Track Inventory" if needed, then edit Re-Order Point, Safety Stock Level, Storage Location, Min Order, Order Multiple, Initial Quantity, and As-Of Date.

  - Once edits are complete, click the blue "Save Product" button at the bottom to apply changes.

4\. **Configure** **Units (Units Tab):**

  - Click on the "Units" tab.

  - If the table is empty, click "New" to add unit conversions.

  - Enter details in columns like Unit, Factor, and Date.

  - Save any additions or edits to define alternative units of measure for the product.

5\. **Add or Edit Suppliers (Suppliers Tab):**

  - Click on the "Suppliers" tab.

  - If the table is empty, click "New" to add a supplier.

  - Fill in columns like \#, Supplier, SKU, Unit, Price, and Date.

  - Save changes to associate suppliers with specific pricing and details for procurement.

6\. **Associate Vehicles (Vehicles Tab):**

  - Click on the "Vehicles" tab.

  - If the table is empty, click "New" to link the product to vehicles.

  - Enter details in columns like Vehicle, Year Model, and Date.

  - Save to specify compatibility or usage with particular vehicle models.

7\. **Review Transactions (Transactions Tab):**

  - Click on the "Transactions" tab.

  - View the history table with columns like Reference No, Transaction Type, Customer/Supplier/Employee, Quantity, Price, Inventory, and Date.

  - Use the filter button to search or sort transactions. This tab is read-only for review.

8\. **Exit and Verify Changes**: After completing maintenance across tabs, navigate back to the main Products list by clicking the browser's back button or the "Products" breadcrumb. Search for the product again to confirm updates in the list view.
