# How to Integrate with Other Modules

Use the following procedure to **utilize the interconnected modules.**

1.  **Access Relevant Modules**: Navigate to the desired module (e.g., Inventory, Services, Vehicles, Reports) from the left-hand menu.

2.  **Enter Data Consistently**: When adding products, services, or vehicle information (e.g., creating a "BRAKE PACKAGE" under Packages or logging a service under Service History), use accurate and consistent details (e.g., customer names, product codes) to enable cross-module linking.

3.  **Update Records**: Modify or add information in one module (e.g., updating a service under Services), and the changes will reflect in related areas (e.g., Reports or Vehicle Transactions Report) automatically.

4.  **Generate Integrated Reports**: Use the Reports menu (e.g., "Sales by Product" or "Vehicle Transactions Report") to pull data from multiple modules. Apply filters (e.g., date range, customer name) and click Generate Report to see consolidated information.

5.  **Export for Review**: After generating a report, click Export to save the integrated data in a desired format for further analysis across modules.
