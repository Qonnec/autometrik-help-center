# How to Manage Pricing Option

Use the following procedure to **update the product pricing.**

1.  Click **Inventory** on the menu.

2.  Select **Products** from the submenu.

3.  **Choose a product** from the list to open its details.

4.  Find the Price Information and **adjust the Purchase Cost** as needed.

5.  **Choose a pricing method** from the dropdown.

<!-- end list -->

  - **FIXED**: Allows you to set a fixed sell price regardless of the purchase cost, providing control over pricing without automatic adjustments. For example, the sell price is manually set to 25.00 PC with a 20.00% margin.

  - **FIXED with OVERRIDE**: Similar to FIXED, but includes an override feature, enabling manual adjustments to the fixed price when needed, offering flexibility for special cases.

  - **MARK-UP 20 W/ OVERRIDE**: Applies a 20% markup on the purchase cost (20.00 PC) to calculate the sell price, with the option to manually override the result if required.

  - **MARK-UP 30**: Adds a 30% markup to the purchase cost, automatically setting the sell price based on this percentage without override capability.

  - **MARK-UP 35:** Increases the purchase cost by 35% to determine the sell price, providing a higher profit margin option.

  - **MARK-UP 35 WHOLESALE**: Applies a 35% markup tailored for wholesale pricing, potentially differing from retail calculations.

  - **MARK-UP 40**: Adds a 40% markup to the purchase cost, suitable for higher-margin products.

  - **MARK-UP 40 W/ AUTO UPDATE**: Applies a 40% markup with automatic updates to the sell price if the purchase cost changes, ensuring real-time pricing adjustments.

  - **Tiered Pricing**: Offers a tiered pricing structure, allowing different sell prices based on quantity or customer type, though specific tiers would need to be configured separately.

6\. Set the Markup Rate percentage to automatically calculate the sell price, or manually enter a Sell Price to see the resulting margin.

7\. **Save changes** by clicking "Save Product."
