# What are Serialized Items

These refer to products that are individually tracked using unique serial numbers. When enabled, each item can be monitored separately for inventory management, including re-order points, safety stock levels, and sales tracking. This is useful for high-value or critical components where individual item history (e.g., purchase date, usage) is important.

Use the following procedure to **enable serialized option.**

1.  Click **Inventory** on the menu.

2.  Select **Products** from the submenu.

3.  **Choose a product** from the list to open its details.

4.  Click the **Serialized** option in Product Information if it is not yet enabled.

5.  **Save changes** by clicking “Save Product.”

Use the following procedure to **track serialized item.**

1.  **Access Inventory Menu**: Click Inventory on the left-hand menu.

2.  **Select Products**: Click Products from the submenu.

3.  **Choose the Product**: Locate and select your desired product from the product list.

4.  **Navigate to Serial Numbers**: Click the Serial Numbers tab under the product details.

5.  **View Existing Serial Numbers**: Check the list of serial numbers (e.g., asa390786321, 32765328635) and their associated dates (e.g., 28 Feb 2025).
