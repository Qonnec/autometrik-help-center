# How to Manage Vehicle Application

It refers to **linking a product to specific vehicles for tracking purposes, such as parts used in repairs or maintenance.** It helps ensure that the correct parts or procedures are matched to the vehicle being serviced.

Use the following procedure to **manage vehicle option.**

1.  Click **Inventory** on the menu.

2.  Select **Products** from the submenu.

3.  **Choose your selected product** from the product list.

4.  **Navigate to the Vehicles** tab.

5.  Enter the following:
    
      - **Vehicle**: Input the vehicle make, model, or VIN (e.g., "Toyota Camry").
    
      - **Year** Model: Specify the vehicle’s model year (e.g., "2023").
    
      - **Date**: Set the date associated with the application (e.g., installation or purchase date).

6.  **Add multiple vehicle entries** if the bulb applies to different vehicles by clicking New and **repeating step 5.**

7.  **Save changes** by clicking "Save Product" (if needed).
