# How to Update Product Information

Use the following procedure to **update the product details**.

1.  **Navigate to Products**: Click on the "Products" option in the left menu.

2.  **Select a Product**: Find the product and click it to open its details.

3.  **Update Information**: Modify the desired fields such as Name, Print Display Name, Description, Category, Manufacturer, Purchase Cost, Sell Price, etc., in the "Product Information" and "Price Information" sections.

4.  **Adjust Inventory Details**: Update inventory details like Re-Order Point, Safety Stock Level, Storage Location, Initial Quantity, or As-Of Date in the "Inventory Details" section if needed.

5.  **Save Changes**: Click the "Save Product" button to save your updates.
