# How to Generate Sales by Product Report

Use the following procedure to **access sales by product report.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Sales By Product**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **Product**, **Product Group**, **Category**, **Manufacturer**, and **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Sales By Product report.
