# How to Generate Reports on Loyalty, Promotions, and Check Registries

Use the following procedure to **access loyalty reports.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Loyalty Points**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate** **Report**" button to view the Loyalty Points report.

Use the following procedure to **access check registries.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Check Register Report**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Check Register report.
