# How to Generate Detailed Job and Invoices

Use the following procedure to **access detailed reports.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Detailed Sales Report**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **Customer**, **Service Advisor**, and **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Detailed Sales Report.
