# How to Generate Purchase Order Report

Use the following procedure to **access purchase orders.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Purchase Order Report**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **Supplier** and **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Purchase Order report.
