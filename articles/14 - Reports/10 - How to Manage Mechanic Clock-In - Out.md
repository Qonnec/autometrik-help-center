# How to Manage Mechanic Clock-In / Out

Use the following procedure to **access mechanic’s time record.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Mechanic Clock In/Out Report**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **Job** **Order**, **Mechanic**, and **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Mechanic Clock In/Out report.
