# How to Generate Statement of Accounts

Use the following procedure to **access statement of accounts.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Statement of Account**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **Customer**, **Format**, and **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate Statement of Account**" button to view the report.
