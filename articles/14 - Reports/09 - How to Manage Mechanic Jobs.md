# How to Manage Mechanic Jobs

Use the following procedure to **access mechanic jobs.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Mechanic Jobs Report**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **Mechanic** and **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Mechanic Jobs report.
