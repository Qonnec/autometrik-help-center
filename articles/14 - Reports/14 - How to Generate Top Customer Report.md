# How to Generate Top Customer Report

Use the following procedure to **access top customers.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Top Customers**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **date range**.

4.  Choose to sort by either "**Count**" or "**Amount**."

5.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Top Customers report.
