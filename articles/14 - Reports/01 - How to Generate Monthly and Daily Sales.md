# How to Generate Monthly and Daily Sales

Use the following procedure to **access sales reports.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Monthly Sales Report**."

3.  **Set the desired date range** by selecting the "From" and "To" dates in the filter section.

4.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Monthly Sales Report.
