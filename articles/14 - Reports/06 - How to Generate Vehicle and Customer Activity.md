# How to Generate Vehicle and Customer Activity

Use the following procedure to **access vehicle transaction.**

1.  On the left-hand sidebar, locate and click on the "**Reports**" section.

2.  From the submenu that appears, click on "**Vehicle Transactions**."

3.  Set the desired filters, such as **Vehicle**, **Customer**, and **date range**.

4.  Click the "**Generate Report**" button to view the Vehicle Transactions report.
