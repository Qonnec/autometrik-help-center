# How to Log In and Out

Use the following procedure to **log in** on your account.

1.  Open Autometrik on your preferred browser.

2.  Enter your **Username** and **Password**, then select **Sign In**.

Use the following procedure to **log out** on your account.

1.  On the upper-right corner of your screen, click the **User Profile section**.

2.  Click **Sign out** to log out.
