# Dashboard Overview

The AutoMetrik Dashboard is **the main page you see when you log into the system**. It gives you a quick and easy look at how your auto shop is doing. Here’s a simple breakdown of what you’ll see:

  - **Job Orders:** This section shows the number of job orders in your shop. It helps you see how many repair or service tasks are active.

  - **Customers**: This part displays the number of customers you’re working with. It keeps track of who’s coming to your shop.

  - **Inventory**: This section shows how many items you have in stock, like parts and accessories, and highlights any that are low.

  - **Vehicles**: This area tells you how many vehicles are in your shop, including those being worked on or waiting.

  - **Profit & Loss**: This gives a quick summary of your shop’s money. It shows your revenue (money coming in) and expenses (money going out) for the month, so you can see if you’re making a profit.

  - **Cash In/Out**: This section tracks the cash moving in and out of your shop, helping you see your daily cash flow.

  - **Accounts**: This part shows your financial accounts, like cash in the bank, to give you an idea of your available funds.

  - **Revenue Breakdown**: This is a chart that breaks down where your money is coming from, like parts sales or service income, so you know what’s bringing in the most.

  - **Expenses Breakdown**: This chart shows where your money is going, like costs for parts or bank fees, helping you manage spending.

  - **6-Month Sales**: This graph shows your sales over the last six months, so you can see trends and how your business is growing.
