# How to Create a SMS Campaign

SMS campaigns are a **bulk or targeted messaging effort sent to multiple customers, often for promotions or announcements**. This allows users to compose a single message, select multiple recipients, and schedule the send (e.g., a promotional campaign launched on the same date). It enhances marketing reach and efficiency by engaging a broader audience with a coordinated message.

Use the following procedure to **create an SMS campaign**.

1.  Navigate to the main dashboard, click **CRM** in the left-hand menu, then select SMS Campaigns.

2.  Click the "**Create SMS Campaign**" button on the SMS Campaigns page.

3.  In the Compose Message step, enter the Campaign Name and type the **SMS Message**, then click "**Continue**".

4.  In the Recipients step, click "**Add Recipients**" to add customers and their mobile numbers, then click "**Continue**".

5.  In the Test & Confirm step, **review the campaign details** in the phone preview, test the message if needed, then choose "**Send Now**" or "**Send Later**" to finalize.
