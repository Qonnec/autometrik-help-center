# How to Manage Service Reminders

Use the following procedure to **access service reminders.**

1.  **Navigate to the CRM** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**Service Reminders**" under the CRM menu to view the list of reminders.

3.  **Review the details** such as Reminder Date, Service, Customer, Mobile No, Job Order, Due Date, Days Due, Last Service, and Status.

4.  To create a new service reminder, click the "**New**" button at the top right.

5.  **Fill in the required fields**: Date, Time, Customer, Vehicle, Service, SMS Message, and optional Notes.

6.  Click "**Add Service Reminder**" to save and activate the reminder.

7.  To edit or delete an existing reminder, use the options available in the list (if provided by the interface).

\[***NOTE*** : Ensure the **Due Date** and **Last Service Date** are **set accurately** to track service schedules effectively.\]
