# How to Send SMS to Customer

Use the following procedure to **send sms to a customer.**

1.  Navigate to the **CRM** section from the left sidebar.

2.  Click on "**SMS Messages**" under the CRM menu.

3.  Click the "Send SMS" button located at the top right.

4.  **Enter the customer's mobile number** in the "Mobile No" field.

5.  **Type your message** in the "SMS Message" text area.

6.  Click the "**Send SMS**" button to send the message.
