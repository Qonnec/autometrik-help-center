# How to Check for Product Availability

Use the following procedure to **check if a product is available.**

1.  **Access Product Inquiry**: Navigate to the "PRODUCT INQUIRY" section from the left sidebar menu.

2.  **Enter Search Criteria**: Input details such as Product Name, Part No., Manufacturer, Category, Supplier, or Storage Location to narrow down the search.

3.  **Initiate Search**: Click the "Search" button to display the list of matching products.

4.  **Review Availability**: Check the "Available" column in the results table, which shows the current stock quantity. Compare this with "On Hand" and "Allocated" columns for a complete view of stock status.

5.  **Adjust Search** (if needed): Refine your search criteria and click "Search" again if the initial results need adjustment.
