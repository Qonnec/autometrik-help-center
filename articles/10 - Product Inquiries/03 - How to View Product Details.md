# How to View Product Details

Use the following procedure to **access product details.**

1.  **Access Product Inquiry**: Navigate to the "PRODUCT INQUIRY" section from the left sidebar menu.

2.  **Click the Product Name**: In the search results, locate the desired product and click on its name to open the detailed view.

3.  **View Product Details**: The "Products / Details" page will display, showing information such as Part No., Product Group, Category, Unit of Measure, Description, Manufacturer, Manufacturer No, Color, Expiry Date, Serialized status, and Display Notes.

4.  **Review Additional Tabs**: Explore tabs like Stocks, Units, Suppliers, Vehicles, and Transactions for more specific information related to the product.

5.  **Edit if Needed**: Make any necessary updates to the details and save changes if applicable.
