# How to Create Purchase Orders

Use the following procedure to **manage purchase orders.**

1.  **Navigate to Purchase Orders**: Access the "Inventory" section and select "Purchase Orders" from the left-hand menu.

2.  **View Existing Purchase Orders**: Browse the table displaying details such as supplier, job order, amount, date, and status, using the "Search" or "Filter" options to locate specific orders if needed.

3.  **Create a New Purchase** Order: Click the "New" button at the top right, enter details including supplier, items required, quantities, and job order, then save the entry to submit.

4.  **Review and Approve** **Orders**: Check the status of each order, approving pending requests by selecting "For Approval" entries and confirming them as needed.

5.  **Track Order Status**: Monitor the progress of purchase orders, updating the status from "For Approval" to "Approved" or "Fully Received" based on delivery and receipt.

6.  **Edit or Update Details**: Locate a specific purchase order, click on it to modify fields like quantity or supplier if changes are required, then save the updates.

7.  **Manage Received Orders**: Verify received goods against the purchase order, marking them as "Fully Received" once confirmed, and address any discrepancies through adjustments if necessary.
