# How to Monitor Supplier Aging and Payment Schedule

Use the following procedure to **manage supplier aging and payment schedule.**

1.  **Access Supplier Aging**: Navigate to the "Accounts Payable" section and select the "Supplier Aging" or "Aging Report" option.

2.  **Generate Aging Report**: Run the report to display outstanding balances categorized by time periods (e.g., 0-30 days, 31-60 days, 61-90 days, over 90 days).

3.  **Review Balances**: Analyze the report to identify overdue amounts and suppliers with significant outstanding balances.

4.  **Set** **Payment Priorities**: Prioritize payments based on due dates, supplier terms, and potential penalties or discounts for early payment.

5.  **Create Payment Schedule**: Develop a schedule in the system, assigning payment dates for each invoice or supplier, ensuring funds are available.

6.  **Update Payment Status**: Record payments as they are made, updating the aging report to reflect reduced balances.

7.  **Monitor Compliance**: Regularly review the aging report and schedule to ensure timely payments and avoid late fees.

8.  **Document Adjustments**: Note any negotiated payment plans or disputes with suppliers in the system for reference.
