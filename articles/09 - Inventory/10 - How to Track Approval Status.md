# How to Track Approval Status

Use the following procedure to **track approval status in POs.**

1.  **Navigate to Purchase Orders**: On the left sidebar, expand the "Inventory" menu and click on "Purchase Orders". This opens the Purchase Orders list page.

2.  **Locate the New Purchase Order**: In the Purchase Orders table, use the search or filter options to find the newly created purchase order. Its status will initially be labeled as "New Request".

3.  **Track Approval Status**: Monitor the status column in the table. An admin must approve the purchase order. Once approved, the status will update to "Approved". You can refresh the page or check periodically to see the updated status.

4.  **Select Received for Approved PO**: Once the status changes to "Approved", open the purchase order by clicking its reference number. Look for a "Received" option or button (typically available in the purchase order details). Click "Received" to mark the order as received, updating the inventory accordingly.
