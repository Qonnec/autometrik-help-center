# How to do Stock Adjustments

Stock Adjustments are special transactions used to manually **add** or **remove** items from your inventory.

**Important:** Stock Adjustments should **only** be used in specific situations, such as:

  - Items are **missing** after a confirmed stock count.

  - Items were **stolen**.

  - Items were given **for free** by suppliers.

  - Items were damaged and cannot be sold.

**Do not** use Stock Adjustments just to “match” the system quantity to your physical count.  
If there’s a difference, first **investigate why** it happened.  
Check the **Product Transactions** tab for the item — it lists all purchases, sales, adjustments, and transfers so you can identify where the discrepancy occurred.

## How Stock Adjustments Affect Inventory
  - **Negative Adjustment** (reducing quantity):
    
      - Decreases the stock count.
    
      - Calculates the deducted value based on your selected costing method (**FIFO** or **Weighted Average**).

  - **Positive Adjustment** (adding quantity):
    
      - Increases the stock count.
    
      - Requires you to enter the **Value per Unit**.
    
      - By default, Autometrik will use the product’s latest purchase cost (excluding VAT) if you don’t enter a new value.

## Editing an Existing Stock Adjustment
1.  Go to the **Stock Adjustments** table.

2.  Click the **Stock Reference Number** for the adjustment you want to edit.

3.  In **Stock Adjustment Info**, update the **Adjustment Reason** or other details.

4.  Modify **Part Number**, **Quantity**, or **Value per Unit** if needed.

5.  Click **Save** to apply changes.

## Adding a New Stock Adjustment
1.  Go to the **New Stock Adjustment** section.

2.  Enter the **Adjustment Reason** (example: “Missing items after count”, “Free items from supplier”).

3.  *(Optional)* Add **Remarks** for internal reference.

4.  Select the responsible employee in **Accounted To**.

5.  Under **Products**, search or type the **Product Name** or **Part Number**.

6.  Set the **Quantity** using **"\<"** or **"\>"**.
    
      - Use a **negative quantity** for removals.
    
      - Use a **positive quantity** for additions.

7.  Enter the **Value per Unit** (excluding VAT) if adding stock.

8.  Click **+ Add Product** to include it in the adjustment list.

9.  Review **Total Quantity** and **Total Value** in the summary.

10. Click:

  - **Create Stock Adjustment** – to save without approving.

  - **Create & Approve** – to finalize immediately.

**Best Practice:  
**Always review transaction history before adjusting stock. Stock adjustments directly affect both your **inventory quantity** and **inventory valuation**, so they should be used sparingly and with proper documentation.
