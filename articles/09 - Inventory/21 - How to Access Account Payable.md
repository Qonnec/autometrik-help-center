# How to Access Account Payable

Account payables (AP) refers to the short-term debts or obligations a company owes to its suppliers for goods or services purchased on credit. It represents money owed to creditors and is tracked as a liability on the balance sheet.

Use the following procedure to **manage accounts payable.**

1.  **Record Invoices**: Enter supplier invoices into the accounting system, noting amounts, due dates, and purchase order references.

2.  **Verify Purchases**: Match invoices with purchase orders and delivery receipts to ensure accuracy.

3.  **Approve Payments**: Review and approve invoices for payment based on company policies.

4.  **Schedule Payments**: Set payment dates, often within agreed credit terms (e.g., 30 days), and allocate funds.

5.  **Process Payments**: Execute payments via check, bank transfer, or other methods, updating the AP ledger.

6.  **Reconcile Accounts**: Compare AP records with supplier statements to resolve discrepancies.

7.  **Report Status**: Generate AP reports to monitor outstanding liabilities and cash flow.
