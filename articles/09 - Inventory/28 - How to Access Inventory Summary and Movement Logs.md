# How to Access Inventory Summary and Movement Logs

**Purpose** of Inventory Movement and Inventory Summary:

  - **Inventory Movement**: Tracks the flow of goods into, within, and out of the inventory, including purchases, transfers, adjustments, releases, and returns. Its purpose is to provide a real-time record of stock changes to ensure accuracy and traceability.

  - **Inventory Summary**: Offers a consolidated overview of stock levels, including On-Hand, Allocated, Available, and On-Purchase quantities, along with valuation details. Its purpose is to give a snapshot of inventory status for decision-making and financial reporting.

**Importance:**

  - **Inventory Movement**: Ensures accurate stock tracking, prevents stockouts or overstocking, supports audit trails, and integrates with accounting for cost management (e.g., FIFO valuation).

  - **Inventory Summary**: Provides visibility into stock health, aids in forecasting, supports budgeting, and ensures compliance with financial standards by reflecting accurate asset values.

Use the following procedure to **manage inventory movement.**

1.  Navigate to the main dashboard, click **Inventory** in the left-hand menu, then select **Inventory Movement**.

2.  **Record all movements** (e.g., purchases, transfers) by entering details like product, quantity, date, and reason.

3.  **Verify movements against purchase orders, receipts, or job orders** to ensure accuracy.

4.  **Review transaction logs** regularly to identify discrepancies and update records as needed.

5.  **Use filters or search to track specific movement history** by product or date.

Use the following procedure to **manage inventory summary.**

1.  Navigate to the main dashboard, click **Inventory** in the left-hand menu, then select **Inventory Summary**.

2.  **Review the summary table for current** On-Hand, Allocated, Available, and On-Purchase quantities.

3.  **Conduct periodic physical stock checks** to reconcile with system data.

4.  **Generate reports to analyze trends and adjust reorder levels or valuation methods** (e.g., FIFO).

5.  **Update the summary after significant movements** (e.g., receipts, releases) to maintain accuracy.
