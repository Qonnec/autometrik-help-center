# How to Manage Stock Releases

Use the following procedure to **manage stock releases.**

1.  **Navigate to Stock Releases**: Access the "Inventory" section and select "Stock Releases" from the left-hand menu.

2.  **View Existing Releases**: Browse the table to see details like release number, date, product, quantity released, and status.

3.  **Create New Release**: Click the "New" button to initiate a stock release, entering the release number, date, and product details.

4.  **Specify Quantity**: Enter the quantity to be released in the "Quantity Released" field, ensuring it does not exceed the "Available Quantity" from "Stock Quantities."

5.  **Assign Destination**: Indicate the department, project, or location receiving the stock in the relevant field.

6.  **Review Stock Levels**: Check the updated "Quantity on Hand" and "Available Quantity" to confirm sufficiency post-release.

7.  **Submit Release**: Save the release details and update the status to "Completed" to finalize the transaction.

8.  **Record Transaction**: Update inventory records to reflect the released stock, ensuring accuracy in the system.
