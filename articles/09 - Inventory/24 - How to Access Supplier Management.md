# How to Access Supplier Management

Use the following procedure to **manage suppliers.**

1.  **Add Suppliers**: Click the "New" button on the Suppliers page to create a new supplier entry. Fill in details such as Name, Code, Contact Person, Contact No, and Date.

2.  **Edit Supplier Details**: Select a supplier from the list and click on "Details" to update information like TIN No, Remarks, Contact Information (Contact Person, Contact No, Fax No, Email Address), Address, Payment Terms, Tax Rate, Tax Inclusive, Merchant Code, and Withholding Tax.

3.  **Save Changes**: After updating or entering supplier information, click the "Save Supplier" button to store the data.

4.  **Track Purchase History**: Use the "Purchase History" tab in the Supplier Details to monitor transactions with each supplier.

5.  **Organize and Search**: Use the search function to quickly find suppliers by name or code, and maintain an organized list for easy access.
