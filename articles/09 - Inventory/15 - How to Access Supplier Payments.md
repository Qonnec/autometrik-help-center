# How to Access Supplier Payments

Use the following procedure to **manage payments to suppliers.**

1.  **Navigate to Supplier Payments**: Access the "Inventory" section and select "Supplier Payments" from the left-hand menu.

2.  **View Existing Payments**: Browse the table displaying details such as payment number, date, supplier, payment method, amount, and status.

3.  **Create a New Payment**: Click the "New" button at the top right to start a new supplier payment.

4.  **Enter Payment Details**: Input the supplier name, date, amount to pay, and select the payment method (e.g., Cash, BDO Check, BPI Check).

5.  **Link Supplier Invoices**: Add relevant invoices by clicking "Add" under "Supplier Invoices," entering invoice number, date, due date, and PO number.

6.  **Add Remarks**: Include any additional notes in the remarks section if needed.

7.  **Submit Payment**: Click "Create Payment" to save the draft or "Create & Complete" to finalize and mark the payment as completed.

8.  **Verify Status**: Check the table to ensure the payment status updates to "Completed" upon successful processing.
