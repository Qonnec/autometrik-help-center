# How to Conduct Physical Stock Checks

Use the following procedure to **physically check stocks.**

1.  **Prepare Inventory List**: Access the "Inventory" section, select "Stock Quantities," and export or print a list of all products with part numbers, descriptions, and current "Quantity on Hand."

2.  **Gather Tools**: Collect necessary items like a clipboard, pen, calculator, and any labels or tags for marking items.

3.  **Assign Team Roles**: Divide the inventory area among team members, ensuring each person knows their section to check.

4.  **Perform Physical Count**: Go through each section, physically count the items, and record the actual quantities on the inventory list, noting any damaged or missing items.

5.  **Compare with System**: Return to the system, navigate to "Stock Quantities," and compare the physical counts with the recorded "Quantity on Hand."

6.  **Identify Discrepancies**: Note any differences between physical counts and system data for further investigation.

7.  **Update Records**: Adjust the "Quantity on Hand" in the system to match the physical count, adding remarks for discrepancies.

8.  **Save and Review**: Submit the updated data and review the "Available" and "Committed" quantities to ensure accuracy.

9.  **Document Findings**: Generate a stock check report summarizing the results and any issues for management review.
