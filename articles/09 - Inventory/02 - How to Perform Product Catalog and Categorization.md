# How to Perform Product Catalog and Categorization

Use the following procedure to **access product inventory.**

1.  **Access the Product Catalog**: Navigate to the "Inventory" section and select "Products" from the left-hand menu.

2.  **View Existing Products**:

<!-- end list -->

  - Browse the table displaying product details.

  - Use the "Search" or "Filter" options to locate specific items if needed.

3**. Add a New Product**:

  - Click the "New" button at the top right.

  - Enter details such as product name, part number, group, category, manufacturer, purchase cost, sell price, and date.

  - Save the entry to update the catalog.

4**. Categorize Products**:

  - Assign a group based on the product type.

  - Assign a specific category to further classify the product within its group.

  - Ensure consistency in categorization for similar products.

5**. Edit or Update Product Details**:

  - Locate the product in the catalog.

  - Click on the product to edit fields like purchase cost, sell price, or category if changes are needed.

  - Save the updates.

6\. **Review and Organize**:

  - Regularly check the catalog to ensure all products are correctly categorized and up-to-date.

  - Adjust categories or groups as the product range evolves.

7**. Utilize for Operations**:

  - Use the categorized catalog for inventory management, pricing decisions, and sales tracking.

  - Export data if needed for reports or analysis.
