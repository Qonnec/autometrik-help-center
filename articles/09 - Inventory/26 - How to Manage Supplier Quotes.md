# How to Manage Supplier Quotes

Use the following procedure to **manage supplier quotes.**

1.  Navigate to the main dashboard, click **Inventory** in the left-hand menu, then select **Suppliers**.

2.  **Select a supplier from the list** and **click** on "**Details**" to access their profile.

3.  Go to the "**Quotes**" tab to view existing supplier quotes or click "**New Quote**" to **create a new one**.

4.  **Enter quote details** such as quote number, date, validity period, supplier name, and items with quantities and unit prices.

5.  **Review the total amount and terms**, then save the quote by clicking "Save Quote."

6.  **Update or delete a quote as needed** by selecting the quote and choosing the appropriate action (e.g., "Edit" or "Delete").

7.  **Use the search or filter options to find specific quotes** by supplier or date.
