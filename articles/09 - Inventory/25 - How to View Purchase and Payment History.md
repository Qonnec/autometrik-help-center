# How to View Purchase and Payment History

Use the following procedure to **view transaction history with supplier.**

1.  **Navigate to Suppliers**: On the left sidebar, expand the "Inventory" menu and click on "Suppliers". This opens the Suppliers list page, displaying a table with columns like Name, Code, Contact Person, Contact No, and Date.

2.  **Select a Supplier**: In the Suppliers table, use the search bar at the top right to find the desired supplier. Click on the supplier's name to open its detailed view.

3.  **View Purchase History**: The page defaults to the "Supplier Info" tab. Switch to the "Purchase History" tab at the top. This displays a table of past transactions with columns like Reference No, Amount, Date, and Status. Review the list, including the total amount at the bottom.

4.  **Access Purchase Order Details**: In the Purchase History table, click on a specific Reference No (e.g., PO0001258). This redirects to the Purchase Order Info page for that transaction, showing details such as Reference No, Date, Tax Inclusive, Status, Supplier, Products (with Part No., Price, Quantity, Received, Amount), and Summary (Sub Total, VAT, Total Amount). Receipts may be listed under a "Receipts" tab or section if available.

5.  **View Receipt Details**: On the Purchase Order Info page, if receipts are associated, click on the "Receipts" tab or a related button (e.g., "View Receipt"). This opens the Purchase Receipt Info, displaying Reference No, Date, Purchase Order No, Status, Supplier, Invoice No, Delivery Receipt No, Delivery Receipt Date, Products (with Part No., Price, Order Qty, Received Qty, Amount), and Summary.

6.  **View Invoice**: From the Purchase Receipt Info or Purchase Order Info page, click on "View Invoice" or a similar button. This generates a printable Supplier Invoice view, including company details, Supplier, Contact Person, Contact No, Parts & Materials (with Item, Part No., Qty, Price, Amount), Subtotal, VAT, and Total. Use options like "View Purchase Order" or "View Payments" for additional related history if needed.
