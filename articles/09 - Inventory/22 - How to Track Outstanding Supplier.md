# How to Track Outstanding Supplier

Tracking outstanding supplier payments in accounts payable refers to **monitoring and managing amounts owed to suppliers for goods or services that have been received but not yet paid.** This involves keeping a record of unpaid invoices, due dates, and payment statuses to ensure timely settlement, avoid late fees, and maintain good supplier relationships. It typically includes reviewing purchase orders, receipts, and invoices to identify outstanding balances and prioritizing payments based on terms like net 30 or net 60.

Use the following procedure to **track supplier balances.**

1.  **Navigate to Accounts Payable**: On the left sidebar, expand the "Inventory" menu and click on "Accounts Payable". This opens the Accounts Payable section, displaying a table with details of outstanding payments.

2.  **Locate Outstanding Supplier Payments**: In the table, use the search or filter options to identify entries related to specific suppliers. Columns may include Supplier Name, Invoice No, Amount Due, Due Date, and Status to help track unpaid balances.

3.  **Review Details**: Click on a specific entry (e.g., an invoice or supplier name) to view detailed information, such as the original purchase order, receipt, or invoice details, including the amount owed and payment terms.

4.  **Track Payment Status**: Monitor the Status column or a related tab to see if payments are pending, overdue, or partially paid. Update or mark as paid once a transaction is settled, if applicable.

5.  **Manage Outstanding Balances**: Use the "Pay Now" option next to an outstanding entry. Clicking "Pay Now" redirects to a new Supplier Payment page.

6.  **Input Payment Details**: On the new Supplier Payment page, enter necessary details such as Payment Amount, Payment Date, Payment Method, and any additional notes or references. Verify the supplier and invoice details pre-filled from the previous step.

7.  **Save the Payment**: After entering details, choose to either click "Create Payment" to save the record with a status of "NEW" or click "Create & Complete" to save the record with a status of "COMPLETE". Upon saving, the user is redirected to the Supplier Payments page to view the updated payment record.
