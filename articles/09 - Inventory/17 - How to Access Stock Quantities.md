# How to Access Stock Quantities

Use the following procedure to **add products in cart that is for purchasing (blue button).**

1.  **Navigate to Stock Quantities**: Open the "Inventory" section from the sidebar and select "Stock Quantities."

2.  **View Product Details**: Ensure the product is listed with its details.

3.  **Open Purchase Cart**: Click the blue "Add to Cart" button next to the product.

4.  **Configure Purchase Cart**:

<!-- end list -->

  - In the "Purchase Cart" popup, verify the product name.

  - Enter the purchase cost.

  - Adjust the quantity using the "+" button.

  - Confirm the unit and amount is calculated correctly.

<!-- end list -->

5.  **Add Supplier (Optional)**: Type in the supplier name in the "Supplier" field if applicable.

6.  **Create Purchase Order**: Click the "Create" button to finalize the purchase order.

7.  **Clear Cart (if needed)**: Click "Clear Cart" to reset the purchase cart.

Use the following procedure to **add products in cart that is for selling (green button).**

1.  **Navigate to Stock Quantities**: Open the "Inventory" section from the sidebar and select "Stock Quantities."

2.  **View Product Details**: Ensure the product is listed with its details.

3.  **Open Sell Cart**: Click the green "Add to Cart" button next to the product.

4.  **Configure Sell Cart**:

<!-- end list -->

  - In the "Sell Cart" popup, verify the product name.

  - Enter the sell price.

  - Adjust the quantity using the "+" button.

  - Confirm the unit and amount is calculated correctly.

<!-- end list -->

5.  **Add Supplier (Optional)**: Type in the supplier name in the "Supplier" field if applicable.

6.  **Create Sell Order**: Click the "Create" button to finalize the sell order.

7.  **Clear Cart (if needed)**: Click "Clear Cart" to reset the sell cart.

\[***NOTE:***

  - Use the "Show Low Qty" and "Low Safety Stock" filters to manage stock levels effectively.

  - The "Search" button can be used to find specific products or details.

  - Ensure stock quantities are updated after creating orders.\]
