# How to Restrict Job Orders from Completing Without Releasing Inventory

If you want to ensure that job orders cannot be completed unless all required parts or inventory items have been released to employees, follow these steps:

1.  **Navigate to Location Settings**: Go to the Location Settings of your site.

2.  **Access Other Settings**: Locate the Other Settings selection within Location Settings.

3.  **Enable Restriction:** Turn **ON** the option labeled **Restrict Releasing Items**.

**Effect of This Setting:** Job Orders will **not be allowed to complete** unless all products have been released. The **Product Releases** page logs which employees have taken the items needed for each job order. This ensures that no job order can be marked complete without properly issuing all required parts to the responsible employees.
