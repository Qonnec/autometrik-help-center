# How to Submit Purchase Order for Review

Use the following procedure to **manage submitted purchase orders.**

1.  **Navigate to Purchase Orders**: Access the "Inventory" section and select "Purchase Orders" from the left-hand menu.

2.  **Create a New Purchase Order**: Click the "New" button at the top right, enter details such as supplier, items required, quantities, and job order.

3.  **Save the Purchase Order**: Submit the entry to generate a draft purchase order.

4.  **Submit for Review**: Locate the saved order in the list, click on it, and select the option to submit it for approval, moving its status to "For Approval."

5.  **Monitor Approval Status**: Check the table to track the order’s progress, ensuring it moves from "For Approval" to "Approved" once reviewed.

6.  **Address Feedback**: If adjustments are requested, edit the order details as needed and resubmit for review.
