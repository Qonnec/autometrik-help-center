# How to Access Supplier Invoices and Linking POs

Use the following procedure to **manage supplier invoice.**

1.  **Navigate to Supplier Invoices**: Access the "Inventory" section and select "Supplier Invoices" from the left-hand menu.

2.  **View Existing Invoices**: Browse the table displaying details such as invoice number, date, supplier, contact number, purchase order (PO) number, due date, amount, and status.

3.  **Create a New Invoice**: Click the "New" button at the top right, enter details including invoice number, date, supplier, and amount.

4.  **Link to Purchase Order**: Select the appropriate PO number from the list or manually enter it to associate the invoice with the corresponding purchase order.

5.  **Review and Verify**: Check the invoice details against the linked PO to ensure accuracy in amount, items, and dates.

6.  **Update Status**: Mark the invoice status as "Open," "Fully Paid," or other relevant states based on payment progress.

7.  **Manage Payments**: Process payments for verified invoices, updating the status to "Fully Paid" once completed.

8.  **Address Discrepancies**: Edit the invoice details if necessary to resolve any mismatches with the PO or delivery records.
