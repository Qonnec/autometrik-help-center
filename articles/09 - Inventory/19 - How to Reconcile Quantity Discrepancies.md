# How to Reconcile Quantity Discrepancies

Use the following procedure to **reconcile quantity discrepancies.**

1.  **Identify Discrepancies**: Access the "Stock Quantities" section, compare the physical count from the stock check with the recorded "Quantity on Hand," and note any differences.

2.  **Investigate Causes**: Review records for potential reasons (e.g., unrecorded returns, theft, or data entry errors) and check related purchase orders or supplier invoices.

3.  **Verify Data**: Cross-check with recent transactions, such as purchase orders, returns, or adjustments, to confirm the discrepancy source.

4.  **Adjust Quantities**: Navigate to the specific product in "Stock Quantities," update the "Quantity on Hand" to match the physical count, and add a note explaining the adjustment.

5.  **Update Related Records**: If applicable, adjust linked purchase orders or invoices to reflect the corrected quantities.

6.  **Recalculate Availability**: Save changes to update "Committed" and "Available" quantities automatically.

7.  **Document Resolution**: Record the discrepancy details and resolution in a log or report for audit purposes.

8.  **Notify Stakeholders**: Inform relevant team members or management of significant discrepancies and actions taken.
