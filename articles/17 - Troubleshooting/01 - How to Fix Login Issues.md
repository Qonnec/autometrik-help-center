# How to Fix Login Issues

Having trouble logging in? Try these solutions:

1.  **Check Credentials:** Ensure you are entering the correct username and password.

2.  **Reset Password:** Use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password.

3.  **Clear Browser Cache:** Clear your browser's cache and cookies if you're using a web app.

4.  **Disable Extensions:** Disable any browser extensions that might interfere with login.

5.  **Contact Support:** If you still can't log in, reach out to our support team for help.
